## common factors and the highest common factor

**A short exploration.**
The factors of $18$ are $1,2,3,6,9,18$; those of $24$ are $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$. Their common factors are $1,2,3,6$, and the greatest is $6$.

:::definition Common factors and highest common factor
A common factor of two or more numbers divides each number. The greatest of the common factors is the highest common factor, written $\operatorname{HCF}(a,b)$.
:::

:::definition Using prime factors
Prime-factorise the numbers, choose the common prime factors with their lowest exponents, then multiply.
:::

**Why does this work?**
A common factor can contain only prime factors present in every number. Its exponent cannot exceed the smallest corresponding exponent; choosing all those smallest exponents gives the greatest common factor.

## Worked examples and applications

:::example Finding the HCF by listing
Find the HCF of $18$ and $30$.

**Solution.**
The common factors of $18$ and $30$ are $1,2,3,6$; the greatest is $6$.

Therefore, $6$.
:::

:::example Using prime factors
Find the HCF of $84$ and $126$.

**Solution.**
We have $84=2^2\cdot3\cdot7$ and $126=2\cdot3^2\cdot7$. Taking common factors with the least exponents gives $HCF=42$.

Therefore, $42$.
:::

:::example Three numbers
Find the HCF of $48,72,120$.

**Solution.**
$48=2^4\cdot3$, $72=2^3\cdot3^2$, $120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$. Their common part is $2^3\cdot3=24$.

Therefore, $24$.
:::

:::example Cutting rope
Two ropes of lengths $84$ m and $126$ m are cut into equal pieces of the greatest possible length. Find the length of each piece and the total number of pieces.

**Solution.**

*Understand.* The piece length must be the greatest common factor of $84$ and $126$.

*Plan.* We find the HCF by prime factorisation.

*Solve.* $84=2^2\cdot3\cdot7$ and $126=2\cdot3^2\cdot7$, so $\operatorname{HCF}(84,126)=2\cdot3\cdot7=42$. The number of pieces is $84\div42+126\div42=2+3=5$.

*Look back.* $42\cdot2=84$ and $42\cdot3=126$, so no rope remains. If more pieces were required, would the length still be $42$ m?

Therefore, each piece is $42$ m long, with $5$ pieces in total.
:::

## Common mistakes and connections

:::warning What can go wrong?
- **Taking every factor present.** That may produce a number that does not divide both originals. The HCF uses common factors with the least exponents. Check the result by division against each original number.
:::

**Connections.** The ideas about common factors and the highest common factor build on earlier learning and will be used in later topics. Notice the link between the data, the rule and the conclusion.

## Summary

- A common factor divides each given number.
- The HCF is the greatest common factor.
- Use the least exponents of common prime factors.
- Problems about greatest equal groups usually use the HCF.
