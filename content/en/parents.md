Many parents hold back from helping with maths because they feel they have forgotten it all. The truth is you do not need to be good at maths. What a child needs most is someone sitting beside them, asking questions and giving them time to think. All you need is one lesson that matches what they are learning, one printed sheet and fifteen minutes a day. Every lesson on Lemonmath is free, follows Vietnam's curriculum and comes in English and Vietnamese.

## Fifteen minutes a day

1. **Pick the lesson.** Open your child's grade below and find the lesson that matches what they are doing at school.
2. **Read it together for 5 minutes.** Each lesson starts with an everyday situation, then explains the idea step by step. Ask your child: "Can you explain it back to me?"
3. **Print the self-study sheet and let them work alone for 10 minutes.** The **Print a self-study sheet** button sits at the top of every lesson. Your child works without looking at the solutions, then checks the answers at the end of the sheet.
4. **Redo it after 1, 3 and 7 days.** The sheet has boxes for this. Spaced practice helps children remember far longer than rereading ([why learn on paper](../learn-on-paper/)).

When your child gets something wrong, try not to rush to the answer. Mistakes are a normal part of learning. Every lesson has a **Mistakes to watch out for** box: read it together and ask whether they made one of those mistakes. A mistake a child finds for themselves stays with them far longer than one someone else corrects.
