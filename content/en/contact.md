Lemonmath always wants to hear from students, parents and teachers. One small suggestion can make a lesson clearer for thousands of other learners.

## Email or Telegram

- **Email:** [support@lemonmath.com](mailto:support@lemonmath.com)
- **Telegram:** +84 919 814 268

We usually reply within a few days. Requests about personal data are answered within 7 working days.

## What do you need?

- **Report a mistake in a lesson.** Tell us the lesson, the grade and what looks wrong (for example "Example 2" or "Exercise 5"). If you use GitHub, you can also [report it here](https://github.com/lemonmathcom/lemonmath/issues).
- **Help with My classes.** Include your account email and the assignment code (6 characters) if you have one. Never send your password or recovery code.
- **See, correct or delete your data.** You can do most of it yourself straight away; the [privacy policy](../privacy/) shows how. For anything else, email or message us as above.
- **Ideas and lesson requests.** Tell us which grade or lesson you would like to see first.
- **Working together.** Schools, learning centres and parent groups who want to use Lemonmath with their students.

Lemonmath will **never** ask for your password, recovery code or learning code by email, message or phone. If someone does, it is not Lemonmath.
