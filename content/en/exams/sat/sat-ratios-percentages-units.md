:::goals
- Split a quantity in a given ratio and solve proportions, including map scales.
- Convert units with conversion factors, including rates such as miles per hour to feet per second.
- Find a percent of a number, a percent change and an original value before a percent change.
- Solve multi-step problems with density, fuel use and mixtures.
:::

Problem-Solving and Data Analysis has about 5 to 7 questions on the test, and roughly half of them are about ratios, rates, percentages and units. These are the most "everyday" questions on the SAT: recipes, maps, prices, discounts and speeds. The math is from grades 6 and 7, but careless reading costs many points, so the habit to build here is to write every quantity **with its unit**.

## Ratios and proportions

A ratio $a:b$ compares two quantities. If a total is split in the ratio $a:b$, the total is made of $a+b$ equal parts.

:::example Splitting in a ratio
In a class of 36 students, the ratio of boys to girls is $4:5$. How many girls are in the class?
A. $16$
B. $20$
C. $9$
D. $25$

**Solution.** There are $4+5=9$ equal parts, so each part is $36\div9=4$ students. Girls: $5\cdot4=20$. The answer is **B**. Option A is the number of boys; option C is the number of parts.
:::

A **proportion** says that two ratios are equal: $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}$, which gives $ad=bc$ (cross-multiplication).

:::example A map scale
On a map, 1 centimeter represents 2.5 kilometers. Two towns are 7.2 centimeters apart on the map. What is the actual distance between the towns, in kilometers?

**Solution.** $\dfrac{1}{2.5}=\dfrac{7.2}{d}$, so $d=7.2\cdot2.5=18$. You type **18**.
:::

## Rates and unit conversion

A rate is a ratio with units, such as kilometers per hour or dollars per kilogram. To convert units, multiply by fractions equal to $1$, such as $\dfrac{5{,}280\text{ feet}}{1\text{ mile}}$, arranged so that the unwanted units cancel. The SAT always gives the conversion factors for US units.

:::example Converting a speed
A car travels at 60 miles per hour. What is its speed in feet per second? (1 mile = 5,280 feet)

**Solution.** Cancel miles and hours:

$$\frac{60\text{ miles}}{1\text{ hour}}\cdot\frac{5{,}280\text{ feet}}{1\text{ mile}}\cdot\frac{1\text{ hour}}{3{,}600\text{ seconds}}=\frac{316{,}800}{3{,}600}=88\text{ feet per second}.$$

You type **88**.
:::

## Percentages

:::theorem Three percent tools
- $p\%$ of $N$ is $\dfrac{p}{100}\cdot N$.
- Percent change $=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{original}}{\text{original}}\cdot100\%$.
- An increase of $p\%$ multiplies by $1+\dfrac{p}{100}$; a decrease of $p\%$ multiplies by $1-\dfrac{p}{100}$.
:::

:::example Percent change
The price of a book rose from 80 dollars to 92 dollars. By what percent did the price increase?
A. $12\%$
B. $13\%$
C. $15\%$
D. $87\%$

**Solution.** $\dfrac{92-80}{80}=\dfrac{12}{80}=0.15$, an increase of $15\%$. The answer is **C**. Option A uses the change in dollars as a percent; option B divides by $92$.
:::

To find an original value after a percent change, **divide** by the multiplier. Never subtract the percent from the new price.

:::example Reverse percentage
After a 20% discount, a jacket costs 64 dollars. What was the price before the discount, in dollars?
A. $76.80$
B. $80$
C. $84$
D. $77$

**Solution.** The sale price is $0.8$ of the original: $0.8P=64$, so $P=80$. The answer is **B**. Option A adds 20% of the sale price, which is the common trap: $64\cdot1.2=76.8$.
:::

:::example A percent of a percent
At a school, 30% of the students are in a sports club, and 40% of the club members are girls. What percent of all the students are girls in the sports club?

**Solution.** $0.4\cdot0.3=0.12$, so $12\%$. You type **12**.
:::

:::warning Common traps
- **Ratio of parts versus ratio to the whole.** In $4:5$, boys are $\dfrac49$ of the class, not $\dfrac45$.
- **Dividing by the wrong number in a percent change.** Always divide by the original value.
- **Undoing a percent by the same percent.** A 20% decrease followed by a 20% increase does not return to the start: $0.8\cdot1.2=0.96$.
- **Dropping units.** Write units at each step; they show whether to multiply or divide.
:::

:::tip Fast percent checks
$10\%$ of a number is easy (move the decimal point), and $5\%$ is half of that. So $15\%$ of $80$ is $8+4=12$. Use this to estimate before you calculate.
:::

## Similar exercises

:::exercise Student-produced response
A sum of 150 dollars is shared between two people in the ratio $3:7$. How many dollars does the person with the larger share receive?

::hint
**What you need:** the total is made of $3+7$ equal parts.

**Steps:**
1. One part is $150\div10$.
2. Multiply by $7$.

::solution
One part is $15$ dollars, so the larger share is $7\cdot15=105$ dollars.
:::

:::exercise Student-produced response
Linh types at a rate of 45 words per minute. How many minutes does she need to type a 1,800-word essay?

::hint
**What you need:** time = amount ÷ rate.

**Steps:**
1. Divide $1{,}800$ by $45$.

::solution
$1{,}800\div45=40$ minutes.
:::

:::exercise Student-produced response
One inch is equal to 2.54 centimeters. What is the length, in centimeters, of a 30-inch screen diagonal?

::hint
**What you need:** multiply by the conversion factor $\dfrac{2.54\text{ cm}}{1\text{ inch}}$.

**Steps:**
1. $30\cdot2.54$.

::solution
$30\cdot2.54=76.2$ centimeters.
:::

:::exercise Multiple choice
The number of visitors to a museum fell from 250 on Monday to 200 on Tuesday. By what percent did the number of visitors decrease?
A. $20\%$
B. $25\%$
C. $50\%$
D. $80\%$

::hint
**What you need:** percent change, divided by the original value.

**Steps:**
1. The change is $50$.
2. Divide by $250$.

::solution
$\dfrac{50}{250}=0.2$, a decrease of $20\%$: answer **A**. Option B divides by $200$; option D is the ratio $\dfrac{200}{250}$.
:::

:::exercise Student-produced response
A total of 54 dollars includes an 8% sales tax. What was the price before tax, in dollars?

::hint
**What you need:** the total is $1.08$ times the price.

**Steps:**
1. Write $1.08P=54$.
2. Divide.

::solution
$P=\dfrac{54}{1.08}=50$ dollars. Check: $50+4=54$.
:::

## Application exercises

:::exercise Density
The density of gold is 19.3 grams per cubic centimeter. A gold block measures 2 centimeters by 3 centimeters by 5 centimeters. What is its mass, in grams?

::hint
**What you need:** mass = density × volume.

**Steps:**
1. Find the volume of the block.
2. Multiply by $19.3$.

::solution
The volume is $2\cdot3\cdot5=30$ cubic centimeters, so the mass is $30\cdot19.3=579$ grams.
:::

:::exercise Fuel for a trip
A car uses 6.5 liters of fuel per 100 kilometers. Fuel costs 1.2 dollars per liter. How much does the fuel cost for a 340-kilometer trip?

::hint
**What you need:** two rates in a chain: liters per kilometer, then dollars per liter.

**Steps:**
1. Liters: $340\cdot\dfrac{6.5}{100}$.
2. Multiply by $1.2$.

::solution
The car uses $340\cdot0.065=22.1$ liters, costing $22.1\cdot1.2=26.52$ dollars.
:::

:::exercise Up and down
A town's population increased by 25% in one decade and then decreased by 20% in the next decade. What was the overall percent change over the two decades?

::hint
**What you need:** multiply the two multipliers.

**Steps:**
1. $1.25\cdot0.8$.
2. Compare with $1$.

::solution
$1.25\cdot0.8=1$: the population is exactly back to where it started, a change of $0\%$. For example, $1{,}000\to1{,}250\to1{,}000$.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
A container holds 40 liters of a salt solution that is 30% salt. How many liters of pure water must be added to make a solution that is 20% salt?

::hint
**What you need:** adding water does not change the amount of salt.

**Steps:**
1. Salt: $0.3\cdot40=12$ liters.
2. Solve $\dfrac{12}{40+w}=0.2$.

::solution
$12=0.2(40+w)\iff 60=40+w\iff w=20$. Add 20 liters of water: the new solution has 12 liters of salt in 60 liters, which is 20%.
:::

:::quiz
::mc
Two numbers are in the ratio $2:3$ and their sum is 45. What is the larger number?
A. $18$
B. $27$
C. $30$
D. $15$
::key B
$45\div5=9$ per part, so the larger number is $3\cdot9=27$. Option A is the smaller number.

::mc
What is 40% of 65?
A. $26$
B. $16$
C. $39$
D. $2.6$
::key A
$0.4\cdot65=26$. Option C is 60% of 65.

::mc
A quantity increases from 50 to 65. What is the percent increase?
A. $15\%$
B. $23\%$
C. $30\%$
D. $130\%$
::key C
$\dfrac{15}{50}=0.3$. Option B divides by $65$; option D is the ratio $\dfrac{65}{50}$.

::mc
A cyclist rides at 15 meters per second. What is this speed in kilometers per hour?
A. $4.2$
B. $25$
C. $900$
D. $54$
::key D
$15\cdot\dfrac{3{,}600}{1{,}000}=15\cdot3.6=54$. Option A divides by $3.6$ instead.

::tf
A pair of shoes costs 120 dollars and is offered at a 25% discount.
a) The sale price is 90 dollars.
b) The discount is 25 dollars.
c) If the sale price is then increased by 25%, the price returns to 120 dollars.
d) The sale price is 75% of the original price.
::key T F F T
a) True: $120\cdot0.75=90$. b) False: the discount is $30$ dollars. c) False: $90\cdot1.25=112.5$. d) True: $1-0.25=0.75$.

::sa
12 is what percent of 48?
::key 25
$\dfrac{12}{48}=0.25=25\%$.

::sa
On a map, 1 centimeter represents 4 kilometers. Two lakes are 6.5 centimeters apart on the map. How many kilometers apart are they?
::key 26
$6.5\cdot4=26$ kilometers.
:::
