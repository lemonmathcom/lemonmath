This is a full-length practice test in the format of the digital SAT Math section: two modules of 22 questions, 35 minutes each. All questions are written by Lemonmath; they are not real SAT questions, but they follow the official content domains and question styles.

## How to take this test

| | Module 1 | Module 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 22 | 22 |
| Time | 35 minutes | 35 minutes |
| Calculator | allowed (Desmos or your own) | allowed |

1. Set a timer for 35 minutes and do Module 1 without stopping. Submit it to see your score.
2. Take a 10-minute break at most, then do Module 2 in another 35 minutes.
3. For student-produced responses, type a number: integers, decimals with a point (for example `0.25`) or a minus sign. On the real test fractions such as `7/2` are also accepted.
4. Afterwards, open the answers, read the explanation of every question you missed and add it to your error log.

On the real test, Module 2 adapts to your performance on Module 1. Here both modules are fixed, and Module 2 is a little harder, like the harder second module.

## Module 1

:::quiz test
::mc
If $5x-3=17$, what is the value of $x$?
A. $3$
B. $4$
C. $20$
D. $70$
::key B
$5x=20$, so $x=4$. Option C is $5x$.

::mc
A store sells pencils at 3 for 1.20 dollars. At this rate, how much do 15 pencils cost, in dollars?
A. $4.50$
B. $5.40$
C. $6.00$
D. $18.00$
::key C
One pencil costs $0.40$ dollars, and $15\cdot0.40=6$.

::mc
Which expression is equivalent to $3(x^2+2x)-x(x-4)$?
A. $2x^2+2x$
B. $4x^2+10x$
C. $2x^2-2x$
D. $2x^2+10x$
::key D
$3x^2+6x-x^2+4x=2x^2+10x$. Options A and C get the sign of $-x\cdot(-4)$ wrong.

::sa
A line in the $xy$-plane passes through the points $(0, 3)$ and $(2, 11)$. What is the slope of the line?
::key 4
$\dfrac{11-3}{2-0}=4$.

::mc
Two angles of a triangle measure $50^\circ$ and $65^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?
A. $65^\circ$
B. $55^\circ$
C. $115^\circ$
D. $245^\circ$
::key A
$180-50-65=65$. Option C is the sum of the two given angles.

::mc
The function $f$ is defined by $f(x)=4x-7$. What is the value of $f(5)$?
A. $3$
B. $20$
C. $27$
D. $13$
::key D
$4\cdot5-7=13$.

::sa
In a survey of 250 students, 36% said they ride a bus to school. How many of the students surveyed ride a bus to school?
::key 90
$0.36\cdot250=90$.

::mc
Which of the following is a solution of $x^2-5x+6=0$?
A. $-2$
B. $-3$
C. $3$
D. $6$
::key C
$(x-2)(x-3)=0$, so the solutions are $2$ and $3$. Options A and B have the wrong sign.

::mc
A gym charges according to $C=25+12m$, where $C$ is the total cost, in dollars, for $m$ months. What does $12$ represent?
A. A one-time sign-up fee
B. The cost per month
C. The total cost after 12 months
D. The number of months
::key B
The number multiplied by $m$ is the rate: 12 dollars per month. Option A describes $25$.

::sa
If $x+y=12$ and $x-y=4$, what is the value of $x$?
::key 8
Adding the equations gives $2x=16$, so $x=8$.

::mc
The function $f$ is defined by $f(x)=3\cdot2^x$. What is the value of $f(3)$?
A. $18$
B. $216$
C. $12$
D. $24$
::key D
$3\cdot8=24$. Option B computes $6^3$; option A computes $3\cdot2\cdot3$.

::mc
The mean of the four numbers 70, 85, 90 and $x$ is 80. What is the value of $x$?
A. $75$
B. $80$
C. $65$
D. $85$
::key A
The sum must be $4\cdot80=320$, so $x=320-245=75$.

::mc
What is the area of a circle with radius 5?
A. $10\pi$
B. $5\pi$
C. $100\pi$
D. $25\pi$
::key D
$\pi\cdot5^2=25\pi$. Option A is the circumference.

::mc
Kim saves 20 dollars each week. She needs at least 300 dollars. Which inequality gives the numbers of weeks $w$ for which she reaches her goal?
A. $20w\le300$
B. $20+w\ge300$
C. $20w\ge300$
D. $w\ge20\cdot300$
::key C
"At least" means $\ge$, and the savings after $w$ weeks are $20w$.

::sa
In the equation $(x+5)(x-2)=x^2+bx-10$, $b$ is a constant and the equation is true for all $x$. What is the value of $b$?
::key 3
$(x+5)(x-2)=x^2-2x+5x-10=x^2+3x-10$.

::mc
Line $k$ is perpendicular to the line $y=-\dfrac13x+2$. What is the slope of line $k$?
A. $-3$
B. $\dfrac13$
C. $3$
D. $-\dfrac13$
::key C
The negative reciprocal of $-\dfrac13$ is $3$.

::mc
In a group of 120 people, 45 prefer tea. Of the people who prefer tea, 18 are under 30 years old. If a person who prefers tea is chosen at random, what is the probability that the person is under 30?
A. $0.15$
B. $0.4$
C. $0.375$
D. $0.18$
::key B
The total is the 45 tea drinkers: $\dfrac{18}{45}=0.4$. Option A divides by 120.

::mc
What is the vertex of the graph of $y=(x+3)^2-7$?
A. $(3, -7)$
B. $(-3, 7)$
C. $(-3, -7)$
D. $(3, 7)$
::key C
$(x+3)^2=(x-(-3))^2$, so the vertex is $(-3, -7)$.

::sa
A right triangle has legs of lengths 9 and 12. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
::key 15
$\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15$.

::mc
The value, in dollars, of a machine $t$ years after it was bought is $V(t)=800(0.95)^t$. Which statement is true?
A. The value decreases by 5% each year.
B. The value increases by 95% each year.
C. The value decreases by 95% each year.
D. The value increases by 5% each year.
::key A
$0.95=1-0.05$: a decrease of 5% per year.

::sa
$$3x+ky=12$$
$$6x+8y=24$$
In the system, $k$ is a constant. The system has infinitely many solutions. What is the value of $k$?
::key 4
The second equation must be twice the first, so $2k=8$ and $k=4$.

::mc
If $x>0$ and $x^2-4x-21=0$, what is the value of $x-3$?
A. $4$
B. $7$
C. $10$
D. $-10$
::key A
$(x-7)(x+3)=0$ and $x>0$, so $x=7$ and $x-3=4$. Option B is $x$.
:::

## Module 2

:::quiz test
::mc
If $2(x+3)=x-4$, what is the value of $x$?
A. $-10$
B. $-2$
C. $2$
D. $10$
::key A
$2x+6=x-4$, so $x=-10$.

::mc
The price of a game was 60 dollars. The price increased by 15%. What is the new price, in dollars?
A. $61.50$
B. $69$
C. $75$
D. $51$
::key B
$60\cdot1.15=69$. Option D is a decrease of 15%.

::sa
If $x^{\frac12}=6$, what is the value of $x^{\frac32}$?
::key 216
$x^{\frac32}=\left(x^{\frac12}\right)^3=6^3=216$.

::mc
For a linear relationship, $y=7$ when $x=1$, $y=13$ when $x=3$ and $y=19$ when $x=5$. Which equation describes the relationship?
A. $y=6x+1$
B. $y=2x+5$
C. $y=3x+7$
D. $y=3x+4$
::key D
The slope is $\dfrac{13-7}{3-1}=3$ and $7=3+b$ gives $b=4$.

::mc
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio $3:5$. The smaller triangle has area 18. What is the area of the larger triangle?
A. $30$
B. $50$
C. $54$
D. $90$
::key B
Areas scale by $\left(\dfrac53\right)^2=\dfrac{25}{9}$: $18\cdot\dfrac{25}9=50$. Option A scales linearly.

::mc
Which of the following is a factor of $6x^2-x-12$?
A. $3x-4$
B. $2x+3$
C. $2x-3$
D. $6x+4$
::key C
$6x^2-x-12=(2x-3)(3x+4)$.

::sa
A car travels 48 miles in 40 minutes at a constant speed. What is its speed, in miles per hour?
::key 72
40 minutes is $\dfrac23$ hour: $48\div\dfrac23=72$.

::mc
The graph of $f(x)=x^2-8x+k$, where $k$ is a constant, has exactly one $x$-intercept. What is the value of $k$?
A. $4$
B. $8$
C. $-16$
D. $16$
::key D
One $x$-intercept means $64-4k=0$, so $k=16$.

::mc
Which equation defines the line that passes through $(2, 5)$ and is parallel to the line $3x-y=4$?
A. $y=3x-1$
B. $y=-3x+11$
C. $y=\dfrac13x+\dfrac{13}3$
D. $y=3x+5$
::key A
$3x-y=4$ is $y=3x-4$, slope $3$. Then $5=6+b$ gives $b=-1$.

::sa
What is the solution of $\sqrt{3x+10}=x+2$?
::key 2
Squaring gives $x^2+x-6=0$, so $x=2$ or $x=-3$. For $x=-3$: $\sqrt1=1\ne-1$, so the only solution is $2$.

::mc
The data set 12, 15, 15, 18, 20, 95 lists the ages of six people at a meeting. If the value 95 is removed, which statement is true?
A. Both the mean and the median decrease, and the mean decreases more.
B. Both decrease, and the median decreases more.
C. Both the mean and the median increase.
D. Neither the mean nor the median changes.
::key A
The mean falls from about $29.2$ to $16$; the median falls from $16.5$ to $15$.

::mc
What is the radius of the circle $x^2+y^2-10x+6y+18=0$?
A. $16$
B. $18$
C. $34$
D. $4$
::key D
$(x-5)^2+(y+3)^2=25+9-18=16$, so $r=4$. Option A is $r^2$.

::mc
A ball's height, in meters, $t$ seconds after it is thrown is $h(t)=-5t^2+30t+2$. What is the maximum height of the ball, in meters?
A. $3$
B. $32$
C. $47$
D. $92$
::key C
The vertex is at $t=3$, and $h(3)=-45+90+2=47$. Option A is the time.

::sa
$$4x+3y=24$$
$$x-3y=-9$$
The solution to the system is $(x, y)$. What is the value of $x+y$?
::key 7
Adding gives $5x=15$, so $x=3$; then $3-3y=-9$ gives $y=4$, and $x+y=7$.

::mc
For nonzero $x$ and $y$, which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{\left(x^3y^{-2}\right)^2}{xy^{-3}}$?
A. $x^4y$
B. $\dfrac{x^5}{y}$
C. $x^5y$
D. $\dfrac{x^6}{y}$
::key B
$\dfrac{x^6y^{-4}}{xy^{-3}}=x^5y^{-1}$.

::mc
A town has 2,000 residents, and the population grows by 3% each year. Which expression gives the population after $t$ years?
A. $2{,}000(0.03)^t$
B. $2{,}000+0.03t$
C. $2{,}000(1.03)^t$
D. $2{,}000(1.3)^t$
::key C
The growth factor is $1+0.03=1.03$.

::sa
For an acute angle, $\sin x^\circ=\cos(2x-30)^\circ$. What is the value of $x$?
::key 40
The angles are complementary: $x+2x-30=90$, so $x=40$.

::mc
An investment is modeled by $f(t)=500(1.44)^t$, where $t$ is in years. Which equivalent form shows the growth rate for each half-year?
A. $500(0.72)^{2t}$
B. $500(1.72)^{\frac t2}$
C. $500(1.2)^{\frac t2}$
D. $500(1.2)^{2t}$
::key D
$1.2^2=1.44$, so $(1.44)^t=(1.2)^{2t}$: 20% per half-year.

::mc
$$kx+2y=5$$
$$3x-4y=1$$
In the system, $k$ is a constant. The system has no solution. What is the value of $k$?
A. $-6$
B. $1.5$
C. $-1.5$
D. $6$
::key C
Parallel lines: $\dfrac k3=\dfrac{2}{-4}$, so $k=-1.5$.

::sa
If $x^2-y^2=40$ and $x-y=4$, what is the value of $x+y$?
::key 10
$(x+y)(x-y)=40$, so $x+y=10$.

::mc
A cone has radius 3 and height 8. What is its volume?
A. $72\pi$
B. $24\pi$
C. $8\pi$
D. $48\pi$
::key B
$\dfrac13\pi\cdot9\cdot8=24\pi$. Option A forgets the $\dfrac13$.

::sa
The equation $x^2+bx+12=0$, where $b>0$, has two solutions that differ by 1. What is the value of $b$?
::key 7
Two numbers that differ by 1 with product 12: $3, 4$ or $-3, -4$. Since $b>0$ the sum $-b$ is negative, so the solutions are $-3, -4$ and $b=7$.
:::
