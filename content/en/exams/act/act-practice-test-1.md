This practice test follows the enhanced ACT Math format: **45 questions, 50 minutes, 4 answer options, calculator allowed, no penalty for wrong answers**. All questions are written by Lemonmath; they are not real ACT questions, but they cover the official content areas at the level of the test. The questions get gradually harder, as on the real test.

## How to take this test

1. Set a timer for **50 minutes** (about 67 seconds per question) and work without looking at the solutions.
2. Make a first pass through all the questions, skip the slow ones, and come back to them.
3. In the last minute, answer every remaining question: a guess cannot lower your score.
4. Submit to see how many questions you got right, then read the solution of every question you missed.

## The test

:::quiz test
::mc
If $3x-7=14$, what is the value of $x$?
A. $7$
B. $\dfrac73$
C. $21$
D. $3$
::key A
Add $7$ to both sides: $3x=21$, so $x=7$.

::mc
What is $25\%$ of $180$?
A. $72$
B. $4.5$
C. $45$
D. $155$
::key C
$0.25\times180=45$.

::mc
A jacket costs $60$ dollars. During a sale, its price is reduced by $15\%$. What is the sale price?
A. $45$ dollars
B. $51$ dollars
C. $9$ dollars
D. $69$ dollars
::key B
The sale price is $60\times0.85=51$ dollars. Option C is the discount, not the price.

::mc
What is the value of $2a^2-b$ when $a=-3$ and $b=4$?
A. $-22$
B. $32$
C. $-14$
D. $14$
::key D
$2(-3)^2-4=2(9)-4=14$. Squaring first keeps the result positive.

::mc
Three numbers are in the ratio $2:3:5$ and their sum is $120$. What is the largest number?
A. $24$
B. $60$
C. $50$
D. $36$
::key B
Write the numbers as $2k$, $3k$, $5k$. Then $10k=120$, so $k=12$ and the largest is $5k=60$.

::mc
The mean of $5$ numbers is $14$. One number, $22$, is removed. What is the mean of the remaining $4$ numbers?
A. $12$
B. $13$
C. $14$
D. $8$
::key A
The total is $5\times14=70$; without $22$ it is $48$, and $\dfrac{48}{4}=12$.

::mc
What is the slope of the line $4x+2y=10$?
A. $4$
B. $2$
C. $5$
D. $-2$
::key D
Solve for $y$: $y=-2x+5$, so the slope is $-2$. Option D is the $y$-intercept.

::mc
Which expression is equivalent to $(x+5)(x-2)$?
A. $x^2-3x-10$
B. $x^2+7x-10$
C. $x^2+3x-10$
D. $x^2-10$
::key C
$x^2-2x+5x-10=x^2+3x-10$.

::mc
Which of the following is equal to $\sqrt{48}$?
A. $3\sqrt4$
B. $16\sqrt3$
C. $4\sqrt3$
D. $4\sqrt{12}$
::key C
$\sqrt{48}=\sqrt{16\cdot3}=4\sqrt3$.

::mc
A rectangle has perimeter $30$ cm and length $9$ cm. What is its area?
A. $54$ cm²
B. $270$ cm²
C. $21$ cm²
D. $63$ cm²
::key A
The width is $\dfrac{30}{2}-9=6$ cm, so the area is $9\times6=54$ cm².

::mc
What are all the solutions of $x^2=6x$?
A. $6$ only
B. $0$ and $6$
C. $-6$ and $6$
D. $0$ only
::key B
$x^2-6x=x(x-6)=0$, so $x=0$ or $x=6$. Dividing by $x$ loses the solution $0$.

::mc
If $f(x)=2x^2-3x$, what is $f(-1)$?
A. $-1$
B. $-5$
C. $1$
D. $5$
::key D
$2(1)-3(-1)=2+3=5$.

::mc
One letter is chosen at random from the word MATHEMATICS. What is the probability that it is a vowel?
A. $\dfrac{4}{11}$
B. $\dfrac{3}{11}$
C. $\dfrac{4}{7}$
D. $\dfrac{7}{11}$
::key A
The $11$ letters include the vowels A, E, A, I: $4$ of them.

::mc
A bus travels $150$ km in $2$ hours $30$ minutes. What is its average speed?
A. $75$ km/h
B. $65$ km/h
C. $60$ km/h
D. $50$ km/h
::key C
$2$ hours $30$ minutes is $2.5$ hours, and $\dfrac{150}{2.5}=60$. Option C treats $2$ hours $30$ minutes as $2.3$ hours.

::mc
What is the area of a circle with radius $7$?
A. $14\pi$
B. $7\pi$
C. $196\pi$
D. $49\pi$
::key D
$\pi r^2=49\pi$. Option A is the circumference.

::mc
If $x+y=10$ and $x-y=4$, what is the value of $xy$?
A. $40$
B. $21$
C. $24$
D. $14$
::key B
Adding the equations gives $2x=14$, so $x=7$ and $y=3$; then $xy=21$.

::mc
What is the sum of the solutions of $|x-3|=5$?
A. $6$
B. $10$
C. $8$
D. $-2$
::key A
$x-3=5$ or $x-3=-5$, so $x=8$ or $x=-2$, and $8+(-2)=6$.

::mc
A right triangle has legs of lengths $9$ and $12$. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
A. $21$
B. $\sqrt{63}$
C. $15$
D. $18$
::key C
$\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15$.

::mc
If $3^x=81$, what is $x$?
A. $27$
B. $3$
C. $9$
D. $4$
::key D
$81=3^4$.

::mc
A line passes through $(2,3)$ and has slope $-1$. What is its $y$-intercept?
A. $1$
B. $5$
C. $3$
D. $-1$
::key B
$y=-x+b$ with $3=-2+b$, so $b=5$.

::mc
Which of the following is the solution set of $-3x+4\le16$?
A. $x\le-4$
B. $x\ge4$
C. $x\le4$
D. $x\ge-4$
::key D
$-3x\le12$; dividing by $-3$ reverses the sign: $x\ge-4$.

::mc
What is the median of $5, 9, 2, 8, 11, 3$?
A. $8$
B. $6.33$
C. $6.5$
D. $5$
::key C
Sorted: $2, 3, 5, 8, 9, 11$; the median is $\dfrac{5+8}{2}=6.5$. Option C is the mean.

::mc
What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $(-4,7)$ and $(6,-1)$?
A. $(1,3)$
B. $(5,4)$
C. $(2,6)$
D. $(1,4)$
::key A
$\left(\dfrac{-4+6}{2},\dfrac{7-1}{2}\right)=(1,3)$.

::mc
What is $(2+3i)(2-3i)$?
A. $-5$
B. $13$
C. $4-9i$
D. $13-12i$
::key B
$4-9i^2=4+9=13$. Option B uses $i^2=1$.

::mc
A pizza shop offers $7$ toppings. In how many ways can a customer choose $3$ different toppings?
A. $210$
B. $35$
C. $21$
D. $343$
::key B
Order does not matter: $\dbinom73=35$. Option A counts ordered choices.

::mc
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio $3:5$. What is the ratio of their areas?
A. $3:5$
B. $6:10$
C. $27:125$
D. $9:25$
::key D
Areas scale by the square of the ratio of sides: $3^2:5^2$.

::mc
What is the vertex of the parabola $y=2x^2-8x+1$?
A. $(-2,25)$
B. $(4,1)$
C. $(2,-7)$
D. $(2,1)$
::key C
$x=-\dfrac{-8}{2\cdot2}=2$ and $y=8-16+1=-7$.

::mc
A town of $8000$ people grows by $5\%$ each year. What is its population after $3$ years?
A. $9261$
B. $9200$
C. $8400$
D. $9600$
::key A
$8000\times1.05^3=9261$. Option A adds $5\%$ of $8000$ three times (simple growth).

::mc
For an acute angle $\theta$, $\sin\theta=0.6$. What is $\tan\theta$?
A. $0.8$
B. $1.33$
C. $0.6$
D. $0.75$
::key D
$\cos\theta=\sqrt{1-0.36}=0.8$, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac{0.6}{0.8}=0.75$.

::mc
If $f(x)=x+4$ and $g(x)=x^2$, what is $g\big(f(-6)\big)$?
A. $40$
B. $4$
C. $-2$
D. $36$
::key B
$f(-6)=-2$ and $g(-2)=4$. Option B is $f\big(g(-6)\big)$.

::mc
What is the $20$th term of the arithmetic sequence $7, 11, 15, \dots$?
A. $87$
B. $80$
C. $83$
D. $147$
::key C
$7+19\times4=83$. Option A uses $20\times4$.

::mc
What is the volume of a cylinder with radius $3$ and height $8$?
A. $72\pi$
B. $24\pi$
C. $48\pi$
D. $192\pi$
::key A
$\pi r^2h=\pi\cdot9\cdot8=72\pi$. Option B is the cone volume.

::mc
In a class of $30$ students, $18$ study French, $12$ study Spanish and $5$ study both. How many study neither language?
A. $0$
B. $10$
C. $5$
D. $7$
::key C
$18+12-5=25$ study at least one language, so $30-25=5$ study neither.

::mc
What is the value of $\log_264-\log_39$?
A. $55$
B. $8$
C. $3$
D. $4$
::key D
$\log_264=6$ and $\log_39=2$.

::mc
What is the distance between $(1,2)$ and $(7,10)$?
A. $10$
B. $14$
C. $\sqrt{28}$
D. $8$
::key A
$\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$.

::mc
In a game, a player wins $5$ dollars with probability $0.3$ and loses $2$ dollars otherwise. What is the expected gain per game?
A. $3$ dollars
B. $0.1$ dollars
C. $1.5$ dollars
D. $-0.1$ dollars
::key B
$5(0.3)-2(0.7)=1.5-1.4=0.1$.

::mc
Which is the equation of the circle with center $(-2,3)$ and radius $4$?
A. $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=16$
B. $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=4$
C. $(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2=16$
D. $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=16$
::key D
$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$ with $h=-2$, $k=3$, $r^2=16$.

::mc
How many real solutions does $2x^2+3x+5=0$ have?
A. $1$
B. $0$
C. $2$
D. infinitely many
::key B
The discriminant is $9-40=-31<0$.

::mc
One pipe fills a tank in $6$ hours and another fills it in $3$ hours. How long do they take working together?
A. $4.5$ hours
B. $9$ hours
C. $2$ hours
D. $3$ hours
::key C
Together they fill $\dfrac16+\dfrac13=\dfrac12$ of the tank per hour, so $2$ hours. Option A averages the times.

::mc
The top of a $40$-m building is seen from a point $30$ m from its base, on level ground. What is the straight-line distance from the point to the top of the building?
A. $50$ m
B. $70$ m
C. $10$ m
D. $35$ m
::key A
$\sqrt{30^2+40^2}=50$ (a $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle scaled by $10$).

::mc
What is the sum of all the entries of $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\4&2\end{pmatrix}$?
A. $14$
B. $10$
C. $6$
D. $12$
::key D
The sum is $\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\4&5\end{pmatrix}$, whose entries add up to $12$.

::mc
If $f(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{3}$, what is $f^{-1}(4)$?
A. $1$
B. $13$
C. $\dfrac13$
D. $11$
::key B
Solve $\dfrac{x-1}{3}=4$: $x=13$. Option A is $f(4)$.

::mc
The value of an account after $t$ months is $V=1500(1.02)^t$ dollars. What does $1.02$ represent?
A. A $2\%$ increase each month
B. The starting value
C. An increase of $1.02$ dollars each month
D. A $102\%$ increase each month
::key A
Each month the value is multiplied by $1.02$, which is a $2\%$ increase.

::mc
A fair coin is tossed $3$ times. What is the probability of getting at least one head?
A. $\dfrac38$
B. $\dfrac12$
C. $\dfrac78$
D. $\dfrac18$
::key C
$1-P(\text{no heads})=1-\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac78$.

::mc
Two positive numbers have a sum of $20$. What is the greatest possible value of their product?
A. $99$
B. $400$
C. $96$
D. $100$
::key D
With numbers $x$ and $20-x$, the product $x(20-x)$ is greatest at the vertex $x=10$: $10\times10=100$.
:::
