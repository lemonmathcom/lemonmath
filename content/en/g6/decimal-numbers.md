One winter morning the weather forecast says: "The temperature on the mountain top is minus $2.4$ degrees Celsius." On a thermometer, the reading is below $0$, somewhere between $-2$ and $-3$. A student swims $50$ m in $34.75$ seconds, and next time in $33.9$ seconds. How many seconds faster was the second swim?

You met decimals in primary school, but then they were all positive. Now that we have negative integers and negative fractions, decimals also get the "other half" of the number line. The good news is that every sign rule you have just learnt for integers still works exactly as before.

## Negative decimals

A fraction whose denominator is a power of $10$ is called a **decimal fraction**, for example $\dfrac{7}{10}$, $\dfrac{-25}{100}$, $\dfrac{-2021}{1000}$. Every decimal fraction can be written as a decimal:
$$\dfrac{7}{10}=0.7;\qquad \dfrac{-25}{100}=-0.25;\qquad \dfrac{-2021}{1000}=-2.021.$$

The numbers $-0.25$; $-2.021$ are **negative decimals**. As before, a decimal has a whole-number part to the left of the decimal point and a decimal part to the right. We read $-2.021$ as "minus two point zero two one".

:::definition The opposite of a decimal
The opposite of the decimal $a$ is $-a$. Two opposite numbers lie on either side of $0$ on the number line, at the same distance from $0$. For example, the opposite of $1.5$ is $-1.5$; the opposite of $-0.08$ is $0.08$.
:::

To change a decimal into a fraction, go the other way and simplify: $-0.125=\dfrac{-125}{1000}=\dfrac{-1}{8}$.

For two positive decimals, we compare as in primary school: the larger whole-number part gives the larger number; if the whole-number parts are equal, compare the tenths, then the hundredths, and so on. For negative numbers, we do the same as with integers.

:::theorem Comparing two decimals
- A negative decimal is always less than $0$ and less than every positive decimal.
- If $a$ and $b$ are positive decimals and $a>b$, then $-a<-b$.
:::

For example, $3.25<3.4$ (because in the tenths place $2<4$), so $-3.25>-3.4$. Once again, the tricky spot is negative numbers: the one that "looks bigger" is actually smaller.

## Adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing decimals

You already know from primary school how to set out the working for the number parts. The only new thing is the **sign**, and the sign follows exactly the rules for integers:

- **Adding two numbers with the same sign**: add the number parts and keep the common sign. **Adding two numbers with different signs**: take the larger number part minus the smaller, and use the sign of the number with the larger number part.
- **Subtracting** means adding the opposite: $a-b=a+(-b)$.
- **Multiplying and dividing**: multiply or divide the number parts; same signs give a positive result, different signs a negative result.

:::note Properties of the operations
Adding and multiplying decimals have all the commutative, associative and distributive properties, just as with integers and fractions. The bracket rule and the order of operations stay the same too.
:::

Why does everything stay the same? Because every decimal is really a decimal fraction. Calculating with decimals is calculating with fractions, just written more briefly.

Two pairs are very handy for quick calculation: $2.5\cdot4=10$ and $1.25\cdot8=10$; also $0.25\cdot4=1$ and $0.5\cdot2=1$.

## Examples

We start by converting between fractions and decimals.

:::example Decimal fractions and decimals
a) Write the fraction $\dfrac{-37}{100}$ as a decimal.

b) Write the decimals $2.05$ and $-0.125$ as fractions in their simplest form.

**Solution.**

a) We have $\dfrac{-37}{100}=-0.37$.

b) We have $2.05=\dfrac{205}{100}$. Since HCF$(205, 100)=5$, we get $\dfrac{205}{100}=\dfrac{41}{20}$.

We have $-0.125=\dfrac{-125}{1000}$. Since HCF$(125, 1000)=125$, we get $\dfrac{-125}{1000}=\dfrac{-1}{8}$.

So a) $-0.37$; b) $2.05=\dfrac{41}{20}$ and $-0.125=\dfrac{-1}{8}$.
:::

Next, comparing and ordering, with both negative and positive numbers.

:::example Comparing and ordering
a) Compare $-3.25$ and $-3.4$.

b) Put these numbers in increasing order: $-1.2$; $0.5$; $-1.25$; $0$; $-0.8$.

**Solution.**

a) Compare their opposites: $3.25$ and $3.4$ have the same whole-number part $3$; in the tenths place $2<4$, so $3.25<3.4$.

Hence $-3.25>-3.4$.

b) The negative numbers are $-1.2$; $-1.25$; $-0.8$. We have $0.8<1.2<1.25$, so
$$-1.25<-1.2<-0.8.$$

The negative numbers are less than $0$, and $0<0.5$.

So in increasing order the numbers are $-1.25$; $-1.2$; $-0.8$; $0$; $0.5$.
:::

Now for adding and subtracting.

:::example Adding and subtracting decimals
Calculate:

a) $(-2.5)+(-1.75)$;

b) $3.2-5.75$;

c) $(-4.6)-(-1.35)$.

**Solution.**

a) Both numbers are negative, so we add the number parts and keep the "$-$" sign:
$$(-2.5)+(-1.75)=-(2.5+1.75)=-4.25.$$

b) Turn it into adding the opposite. The signs are different and $5.75>3.2$, so the result is negative:
$$3.2-5.75=3.2+(-5.75)=-(5.75-3.2)=-2.55.$$

c) The opposite of $-1.35$ is $1.35$. The signs are different and $4.6>1.35$, so the result is negative:
$$(-4.6)-(-1.35)=(-4.6)+1.35=-(4.6-1.35)=-3.25.$$

So the results are $-4.25$; $-2.55$; $-3.25$.
:::

Multiplying and dividing need just one extra step: decide the sign first.

:::example Multiplying and dividing decimals
Calculate:

a) $(-1.25)\cdot0.8$;

b) $(-3.6):(-0.12)$;

c) $0.45\cdot(-20)$.

**Solution.**

a) The signs are different, so the product is negative. We have $1.25\cdot0.8=1$, so
$$(-1.25)\cdot0.8=-1.$$

b) The signs are the same, so the quotient is positive. We have $3.6:0.12=360:12=30$, so
$$(-3.6):(-0.12)=30.$$

c) The signs are different, so the product is negative. We have $0.45\cdot20=9$, so
$$0.45\cdot(-20)=-9.$$

So the results are $-1$; $30$; $-9$.
:::

The properties help us avoid long multiplications.

:::example Calculating in a sensible order
Calculate:

a) $2.5\cdot(-0.73)\cdot4$;

b) $(-3.7)\cdot2.6+(-3.7)\cdot7.4$.

**Solution.**

a) Using the commutative and associative properties, we group $2.5$ with $4$:
$$2.5\cdot(-0.73)\cdot4=(2.5\cdot4)\cdot(-0.73)=10\cdot(-0.73)=-7.3.$$

b) The two products share the factor $-3.7$. Using the distributive property:
$$(-3.7)\cdot2.6+(-3.7)\cdot7.4=(-3.7)\cdot(2.6+7.4)=(-3.7)\cdot10=-37.$$

So a) $-7.3$; b) $-37$.
:::

Finally, back to the temperature on the mountain.

:::example Temperature through a day
At $6$ a.m. the temperature on the mountain top is $-2.4^\circ\text{C}$. By midday it has risen by $6.5^\circ\text{C}$. By the evening it has fallen by $3.8^\circ\text{C}$ from the midday temperature. Find the temperature on the mountain top in the evening.

**Solution.**

*Understand.* The starting temperature is negative. A rise means adding, a fall means subtracting. We need the final temperature.

*Plan.* Write the whole process as one expression and work it out.

*Solve.* The temperature on the mountain top in the evening is
$$\begin{aligned}
-2.4+6.5-3.8&=(6.5-2.4)-3.8\\
&=4.1-3.8\\
&=0.3\ (^\circ\text{C}).
\end{aligned}$$

So in the evening the temperature on the mountain top is $0.3^\circ\text{C}$.

*Look back.* Is there another way? Over the day the temperature rises by $6.5$ and falls by $3.8$, an overall rise of $6.5-3.8=2.7^\circ\text{C}$. Then $-2.4+2.7=0.3$, the same result.
:::

:::warning Common mistakes
- **Comparing negative decimals like positive ones.** Wrong: $-3.4>-3.25$ "because $3.4>3.25$". Why it is wrong: for negative numbers, the one with the larger number part is smaller. Right: $-3.4<-3.25$.
- **Comparing by counting digits.** Wrong: $3.25>3.4$ "because $325>34$". Why it is wrong: compare place by place from left to right, starting with the whole-number part. Right: in the tenths place $2<4$, so $3.25<3.4$.
- **Forgetting the sign when subtracting.** Wrong: $3.2-5.75=2.55$. Why it is wrong: the number subtracted is larger, so the difference must be negative. Right: $3.2-5.75=-2.55$.
- **Putting the decimal point in the wrong place when dividing.** Wrong: $3.6:0.12=3$. Why it is wrong: the decimal point must move the same number of places in both numbers: $360:12$. Right: $3.6:0.12=30$.
:::

## Summary

- A decimal fraction (denominator a power of $10$) can be written as a decimal: $\dfrac{-25}{100}=-0.25$. The other way, write the decimal as a fraction and simplify.
- The opposite of $a$ is $-a$. A negative decimal is less than $0$ and less than every positive number; if $a>b>0$, then $-a<-b$.
- Adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing decimals: the number parts are worked as in primary school, and the sign follows the rules for integers.
- The commutative, associative and distributive properties and the bracket rule still hold; remember $2.5\cdot4=10$; $1.25\cdot8=10$; $0.25\cdot4=1$.

The swimmer from the opening was $34.75-33.9=0.85$ seconds faster. As for $-2.4^\circ\text{C}$ on the mountain top, you now know where it sits and can work out how it changes during the day. In the next lesson we learn [rounding and estimating](lesson:rounding-and-estimating), a skill grown-ups use every day when shopping.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
Write these numbers as decimals: $\dfrac{-7}{10}$; $\dfrac{13}{100}$; $\dfrac{-2021}{1000}$; $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

::hint
**What you need:** a decimal fraction can be written as a decimal straight away; other fractions must first be changed into decimal fractions.

**Steps:**
1. For the first three fractions: count the zeros in the denominator.
2. For $\dfrac34$: multiply the numerator and the denominator by $25$.

::solution
We have $\dfrac{-7}{10}=-0.7$; $\dfrac{13}{100}=0.13$; $\dfrac{-2021}{1000}=-2.021$.

We have $\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{3\cdot25}{4\cdot25}=\dfrac{75}{100}=0.75$.

So the decimals are $-0.7$; $0.13$; $-2.021$; $0.75$.

**Answer.** $-0.7$; $0.13$; $-2.021$; $0.75$.
:::

:::exercise
Write these decimals as fractions in their simplest form: $0.6$; $-1.25$; $-0.08$.

::hint
**What you need:** write as a decimal fraction, then simplify using the HCF.

**Steps:**
1. Count the digits after the decimal point to choose the denominator $10$, $100$, …
2. Simplify.

::solution
We have $0.6=\dfrac{6}{10}=\dfrac{3}{5}$.

We have $-1.25=\dfrac{-125}{100}$. Since HCF$(125, 100)=25$, we get $\dfrac{-125}{100}=\dfrac{-5}{4}$.

We have $-0.08=\dfrac{-8}{100}$. Since HCF$(8, 100)=4$, we get $\dfrac{-8}{100}=\dfrac{-2}{25}$.

So $0.6=\dfrac35$; $-1.25=\dfrac{-5}{4}$; $-0.08=\dfrac{-2}{25}$.

**Answer.** $\dfrac35$; $\dfrac{-5}{4}$; $\dfrac{-2}{25}$.
:::

:::exercise
Compare:

a) $-5.08$ and $-5.8$;

b) $-0.999$ and $0.01$;

c) $-12.3$ and $-12.30$.

::hint
**What you need:** a negative number is less than a positive one; if $a>b>0$, then $-a<-b$; adding a zero at the end of the decimal part does not change a number.

**Steps:**
1. Look at the signs of the two numbers.
2. If both are negative, compare their opposites.

::solution
a) We compare $5.08$ and $5.8$: same whole-number part $5$; in the tenths place $0<8$, so $5.08<5.8$. Hence $-5.08>-5.8$.

b) $-0.999$ is negative and $0.01$ is positive. Hence $-0.999<0.01$.

c) We have $12.30=12.3$, so $-12.3=-12.30$.

So a) $-5.08>-5.8$; b) $-0.999<0.01$; c) $-12.3=-12.30$.

**Answer.** a) $>$; b) $<$; c) $=$.
:::

:::exercise
Put these numbers in decreasing order: $2.1$; $-3.05$; $-3.5$; $0$; $-0.7$; $2.01$.

::hint
**What you need:** positive numbers are greater than $0$, negative numbers are less than $0$.

**Steps:**
1. Order the positive numbers, then $0$.
2. Order the negative numbers: the one with the smaller number part is the larger.

::solution
The positive numbers are $2.1$ and $2.01$. In the tenths place $1>0$, so $2.1>2.01$.

$0$ is less than the positive numbers and greater than the negative numbers.

The negative numbers are $-3.05$; $-3.5$; $-0.7$. We have $0.7<3.05<3.5$, so $-0.7>-3.05>-3.5$.

So in decreasing order the numbers are $2.1$; $2.01$; $0$; $-0.7$; $-3.05$; $-3.5$.

**Answer.** $2.1$; $2.01$; $0$; $-0.7$; $-3.05$; $-3.5$.
:::

:::exercise
Calculate:

a) $(-6.25)+4.8$;

b) $-3.7-2.45$;

c) $1.2-(-0.85)$.

::hint
**What you need:** the rules for adding numbers with the same sign and with different signs; $a-b=a+(-b)$.

**Steps:**
1. Turn subtraction into adding the opposite.
2. Decide the sign, then work out the number part.

::solution
a) The signs are different and $6.25>4.8$, so the result is negative:
$$(-6.25)+4.8=-(6.25-4.8)=-1.45.$$

b) We have $-3.7-2.45=(-3.7)+(-2.45)$. Both numbers are negative, so
$$(-3.7)+(-2.45)=-(3.7+2.45)=-6.15.$$

c) The opposite of $-0.85$ is $0.85$, so
$$1.2-(-0.85)=1.2+0.85=2.05.$$

So the results are $-1.45$; $-6.15$; $2.05$.

**Answer.** a) $-1.45$; b) $-6.15$; c) $2.05$.
:::

:::exercise
Calculate:

a) $(-0.25)\cdot(-1.6)$;

b) $4.5:(-0.15)$;

c) $(-2.4)\cdot0.5-1.8:0.6$.

::hint
**What you need:** same signs give a positive product or quotient, different signs a negative one. Multiply and divide first, then add and subtract.

**Steps:**
1. Decide the sign of each product or quotient.
2. In part c), work out the product and the quotient before subtracting.

::solution
a) The signs are the same, so the product is positive:
$$(-0.25)\cdot(-1.6)=0.25\cdot1.6=0.4.$$

b) The signs are different, so the quotient is negative. We have $4.5:0.15=450:15=30$, so
$$4.5:(-0.15)=-30.$$

c) Multiply and divide first:
$$(-2.4)\cdot0.5=-1.2;\qquad 1.8:0.6=3.$$

Hence
$$(-2.4)\cdot0.5-1.8:0.6=-1.2-3=-4.2.$$

So the results are $0.4$; $-30$; $-4.2$.

**Answer.** a) $0.4$; b) $-30$; c) $-4.2$.
:::

:::exercise
Calculate in a sensible order:

a) $12.75+(-4.3)+(-12.75)+6.3$;

b) $0.25\cdot(-7.9)\cdot40$.

::hint
**What you need:** opposite numbers add up to $0$; $0.25\cdot40=10$.

**Steps:**
1. Part a): group the two opposite numbers.
2. Part b): group $0.25$ with $40$.

::solution
a) Using the commutative and associative properties:
$$\begin{aligned}
12.75+(-4.3)+(-12.75)+6.3&=[12.75+(-12.75)]+[(-4.3)+6.3]\\
&=0+2=2.
\end{aligned}$$

b) We group $0.25$ with $40$:
$$0.25\cdot(-7.9)\cdot40=(0.25\cdot40)\cdot(-7.9)=10\cdot(-7.9)=-79.$$

So a) $2$; b) $-79$.

**Answer.** a) $2$; b) $-79$.
:::

:::exercise
The water level of a reservoir is recorded relative to its normal level (a level below normal is recorded as a negative number). At the end of January the level is $-0.35$ m. During February it falls by another $0.4$ m. During March, thanks to rain, it rises by $1.25$ m. At the end of March, is the water level above or below normal, and by how many metres?

::hint
**What you need:** a fall means subtracting, a rise means adding; a positive result means above normal.

**Steps:**
1. Write the expression $-0.35-0.4+1.25$.
2. Work it out and explain the sign of the result.

::solution
The water level at the end of March, relative to normal, is
$$\begin{aligned}
-0.35-0.4+1.25&=(-0.35)+(-0.4)+1.25\\
&=(-0.75)+1.25\\
&=0.5\ (\text{m}).
\end{aligned}$$

The result is positive, so the level is above normal.

So at the end of March the water level is $0.5$ m above normal.

**Answer.** $0.5$ m above normal.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Work out quickly:
$$(-1.5)+2.5+(-3.5)+4.5+(-5.5)+6.5+(-7.5)+8.5+(-9.5)+10.5.$$

::hint
**What you need:** the associative property; group the terms in pairs.

**Steps:**
1. Work out the first pair $(-1.5)+2.5$.
2. Count how many such pairs there are.

::solution
We group the terms in consecutive pairs:
$$[(-1.5)+2.5]+[(-3.5)+4.5]+[(-5.5)+6.5]+[(-7.5)+8.5]+[(-9.5)+10.5].$$

Each pair adds up to $1$; for example, $(-1.5)+2.5=1$ and $(-9.5)+10.5=1$.

There are $5$ pairs, so the sum is
$$1+1+1+1+1=5.$$

So the value of the expression is $5$.

**Answer.** $5$.
:::

::key A
The opposite of $-3.07$ is $3.07$. B is the number itself; C drops the zero; D moves the decimal point.

::key C
