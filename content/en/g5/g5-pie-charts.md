## Pie charts

The whole circle stands for **all** $40$ pupils, which is $100\%$. Each sector is a part of the circle. The bigger the sector, the bigger the group.

Look at the chart: football takes exactly half of the circle, so it is $50\%$. Badminton takes a quarter of the circle, so it is $25\%$. Add them up: $50+25+15+10=100$, which fills the whole circle.

:::theorem Reading a pie chart
In a pie chart:
1. The whole circle is $100\%$, which is the total number of things surveyed.
2. To find the amount in a group, divide the total by $100$ and multiply by the percentage of that group.
:::

This works because $1\%$ of the total is the total divided by $100$. A group with $25\%$ has $25$ lots of that. Remember: $25\%$ of $40$ is $10$, not $25$.

:::example The number of pupils for each sport
Use the chart at the start to find how many pupils chose each sport.

**Solution.** The whole circle is $40$ pupils. The number choosing each sport is:
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Football: }&40\div100\times50=20\ (\text{pupils}),\\ \text{Badminton: }&40\div100\times25=10\ (\text{pupils}),\\ \text{Swimming: }&40\div100\times15=6\ (\text{pupils}),\\ \text{Other: }&40\div100\times10=4\ (\text{pupils}). \end{aligned}$$
**Check.** $20+10+6+4=40$. Correct.

**Answer.** Football $20$ pupils; badminton $10$ pupils; swimming $6$ pupils; other sports $4$ pupils.
:::

The other way round, from the data we find the percentages to draw the chart.

:::example Finding a percentage
$6$ pupils out of $40$ chose swimming. Find the percentage of the class that chose swimming.

**Solution.** We divide $6$ by $40$ and write the result as a percentage:
$$6\div40=0.15=15\%.$$
So $6$ pupils make up $15\%$ of the class.
:::

:::example Combining two groups
What percentage of the pupils chose badminton or swimming? How many pupils is that?

**Solution.** We add the two percentages:
$$25\%+15\%=40\%.$$
The number of pupils is $40\div100\times40=16$ (pupils).

**Check.** Badminton has $10$ pupils and swimming has $6$ pupils, and $10+6=16$. Correct.

So $40\%$ of the pupils, that is $16$ pupils.
:::

## Relative frequency of an event

Nam tosses a coin $50$ times and records which side lands face up each time. Heads came up $27$ times. The event "heads" is repeated $27$ times out of $50$ tosses in total.

We write the ratio $27:50=\dfrac{27}{50}=\dfrac{54}{100}=54\%$. So heads came up in $54\%$ of the tosses.

:::theorem Relative frequency of an event
The relative frequency of an event, the number of times it happens compared with the total number of trials, is
$$\text{number of times the event happens}:\text{total number of trials}.$$
You can write this ratio as a fraction, a decimal or a percentage.
:::

This ratio does not have to be $50\%$. Nam only tossed the coin $50$ times, and every toss can give heads or tails. Notice that the total number of trials always comes **after** the division sign.

:::example Tossing a coin
Nam tosses a coin $50$ times and gets heads $27$ times. Find the percentage of tosses that gave heads and the percentage that gave tails.

**Solution.** Heads came up $27$ times, so the ratio is $27:50=0.54=54\%$. Tails came up $50-27=23$ (times), so the ratio is $23:50=0.46=46\%$.

**Check.** $54\%+46\%=100\%$. Correct.

**Answer.** Heads $54\%$; tails $46\%$.
:::

The next problem has more outcomes, so we use *Understand, Plan, Solve, Look back*.

:::example Rolling a die
Mai rolls a die $50$ times and records the number on the top face: $1$ came up $7$ times, $2$ came up $9$ times, $3$ came up $8$ times, $4$ came up $12$ times, $5$ came up $6$ times and $6$ came up $8$ times. Find the percentage for each face. Which face came up most often?

**Solution.**

*Understand.* The total number of rolls is $50$. We are given how many times each of the six faces came up and we need the percentage for each face.

*Plan.* The ratio for each face is the number of times it came up divided by $50$, written as a percentage.

*Solve.*
$$\begin{aligned} 7\div50&=0.14=14\%, & 9\div50&=0.18=18\%, & 8\div50&=0.16=16\%,\\ 12\div50&=0.24=24\%, & 6\div50&=0.12=12\%, & 8\div50&=0.16=16\%. \end{aligned}$$
The face $4$ has the greatest ratio ($24\%$).

*Look back.* The ratios add up to $14+18+16+24+12+16=100$, so the result makes sense. A fair die has $6$ faces, so each face takes about $\dfrac16\approx16.7\%$. Mai rolled only a few times, so the ratios differ; with many more rolls, the ratios are usually closer to $16.7\%$.

**Answer.** $14\%$; $18\%$; $16\%$; $24\%$; $12\%$; $16\%$. The face $4$ came up most often.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Treating a percentage as an amount.** Wrong: the chart says $25\%$, so $25$ pupils chose badminton. Why it is wrong: $25\%$ means $25$ parts out of $100$ parts of the $40$ pupils. Right: $40\div100\times25=10$ pupils.
- **Forgetting to write the ratio out of $100$.** Wrong: the percentage of $27$ out of $50$ is $27\%$. Why it is wrong: $27\div50=0.54$. Right: $54\%$.
- **Writing the ratio the wrong way round.** Wrong: the ratio of heads to the total number of tosses is $50:27$. Why it is wrong: the total must come after the division sign. Right: $27:50$.
:::

## Summary

- The whole circle of a pie chart is $100\%$ of the total; the bigger the sector, the bigger the group.
- The amount in a group is the total divided by $100$ and multiplied by the group's percentage.
- The relative frequency of an event is the number of times it happens : the total number of trials.
- Check by adding: all the percentages must make $100\%$.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
A pie chart shows how $200$ pupils in Year $5$ travel to school: by bicycle $40\%$, on foot $35\%$, by motorbike (taken by parents) $25\%$. Find the number of pupils for each way.

::hint
**What you need:** the whole circle is $100\%$, which stands for $200$ pupils.

**Steps:**
1. How many pupils is $1\%$ of $200$?
2. Multiply by the percentage for each group.

::solution
By bicycle: $200\div100\times40=80$ (pupils). On foot: $200\div100\times35=70$ (pupils). By motorbike: $200\div100\times25=50$ (pupils).

**Check.** $80+70+50=200$.

**Answer.** $80$ pupils; $70$ pupils; $50$ pupils.
:::

:::exercise
In a chart of favourite kinds of book: comics $45\%$, stories $30\%$, science books $15\%$, and the rest are other kinds. What percentage are other kinds?

::hint
**What you need:** all the sectors add up to $100\%$.

**Steps:**
1. Add the three known percentages.
2. Subtract the total from $100\%$.

::solution
The three known kinds make $45+30+15=90\ (\%)$. Other kinds make $100-90=10\ (\%)$.

**Answer.** $10\%$.
:::

:::exercise
Of $25$ children, $7$ like drawing. Find the percentage of the group who like drawing.

::hint
**What you need:** the percentage of $a$ out of $b$ is $a:b$ written as a percentage.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $7:25$.
2. Write it as a percentage.

::solution
$7:25=0.28=28\%$.

**Answer.** $28\%$.
:::

:::exercise
A school collects $50$ kg of recycling: $30$ kg of paper, $15$ kg of plastic and $5$ kg of metal. Find the percentage of each kind out of the total mass.

::hint
**What you need:** percentage $=$ mass of each kind : total mass.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. Work out each ratio.
3. Check: do the three percentages make $100\%$?

::solution
Paper: $30:50=0.6=60\%$. Plastic: $15:50=0.3=30\%$. Metal: $5:50=0.1=10\%$.

**Check.** $60+30+10=100$.

**Answer.** Paper $60\%$; plastic $30\%$; metal $10\%$.
:::

:::exercise
An's family spends $10$ million dong a month. The chart shows: food $40\%$, education $20\%$, water and electricity $10\%$, and the rest is saved. Find the amount for each item.

::hint
**What you need:** amount for each item $=$ total : $100\times$ percentage.

**Steps:**
1. Find the percentage saved.
2. Find the amount for each item.

::solution
Savings are $100-(40+20+10)=30\ (\%)$. The amounts (in million dong) are:
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Food: }&10\div100\times40=4,\\ \text{Education: }&10\div100\times20=2,\\ \text{Water and electricity: }&10\div100\times10=1,\\ \text{Savings: }&10\div100\times30=3. \end{aligned}$$
**Answer.** Food $4$ million dong; education $2$ million dong; water and electricity $1$ million dong; savings $3$ million dong.
:::

:::exercise
Ha tosses a coin $40$ times and gets heads $18$ times. Find the percentage of tosses that gave heads and the percentage that gave tails.

::hint
**What you need:** ratio $=$ number of times the event happens : total number of trials.

**Steps:**
1. Find the number of tails.
2. Work out both ratios and add them to check.

::solution
Heads: $18:40=0.45=45\%$. Tails came up $40-18=22$ (times): $22:40=0.55=55\%$.

**Answer.** Heads $45\%$; tails $55\%$.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the lesson on percentages. a) Find $35\%$ of $240$. b) $15\%$ of a number is $36$. Find the number.

::hint
**What you need:** $1\%$ of a number is the number divided by $100$.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: $240\div100\times35$.
2. Part b: find $1\%$, then multiply by $100$.

::solution
a) $240\div100\times35=84$.

b) $1\%$ of the number is $36\div15=2.4$. The number is $2.4\times100=240$.

**Answer.** a) $84$; b) $240$.
:::

:::exercise
A box holds red and blue marbles. Hung picks a marble at random, notes its colour and puts it back, $30$ times. He gets a red marble $12$ times. Find the relative frequency of red marbles as a fraction in its simplest form and as a percentage.

::hint
**What you need:** ratio $=$ number of times a red marble was picked : total number of picks.

**Steps:**
1. Write $12:30$ as a fraction and simplify it.
2. Change it to a percentage.

::solution
$12:30=\dfrac{12}{30}=\dfrac25=0.4=40\%$.

**Answer.** $\dfrac25$; $40\%$.
:::

:::exercise
$80$ pupils were asked about their favourite subject: maths $35\%$, Vietnamese $25\%$, art $20\%$, and the rest chose English. Which subject was chosen most often? How many more pupils chose it than the least popular subject?

::hint
**What you need:** number of pupils $=80\div100\times$ percentage.

**Steps:**
1. Find the percentage for English.
2. Find the number for each subject and compare.

::solution
English has $100-(35+25+20)=20\ (\%)$. The numbers of pupils are: maths $80\div100\times35=28$; Vietnamese $80\div100\times25=20$; art $80\div100\times20=16$; English $80\div100\times20=16$.

Maths was chosen most often. The least popular subjects have $16$ pupils, and $28-16=12$.

**Answer.** Maths; $12$ more pupils.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
A box holds red and blue marbles. Hung picks a marble at random, notes its colour and puts it back, $40$ times, and gets a red marble $10$ times. Using this result, estimate how many red marbles there are if the box holds $200$ marbles.

::hint
**What you need:** the relative frequency of red marbles gives an estimate of the percentage of red marbles in the box.

**Steps:**
1. Find the percentage of picks that were red.
2. Take that percentage of $200$.

::solution
The relative frequency of red marbles is $10:40=0.25=25\%$. We estimate the number of red marbles as $200\div100\times25=50$ (marbles).

This is only an estimate; the true number of red marbles may be a little different.

**Answer.** About $50$ red marbles.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
A pie chart shows that $25\%$ of $40$ pupils chose badminton. The number of pupils who chose badminton is
A. $25$.
B. $1000$.
C. $16$.
D. $10$.
::key D
$40\div100\times25=10$. Option A treats $25\%$ as $25$ pupils, C is $40\%$ of $40$ and B forgets to divide by $100$.

::mc
The percentage that $8$ is of $32$ is
A. $25\%$.
B. $32\%$.
C. $400\%$.
D. $8\%$.
::key A
$8:32=0.25=25\%$. Option D writes the number as a percentage, B takes the total and C is $32:8=4$.

::mc
A coin is tossed $20$ times and lands heads $13$ times. The percentage of tosses that gave heads is
A. $13\%$.
B. $65\%$.
C. $35\%$.
D. $\dfrac{20}{13}$.
::key B
$13:20=0.65=65\%$. Option A writes the number of times as a percentage, C is the ratio for tails and D reverses the ratio.

::mc
Three sectors of a pie chart take $40\%$, $35\%$ and $15\%$. The remaining sector takes
A. $90\%$.
B. $5\%$.
C. $10\%$.
D. $100\%$.
::key C
$40+35+15=90$, so the rest is $100-90=10\ (\%)$. Option A is the total of the three sectors and D is the whole circle.

::tf
A survey of $40$ pupils gives: football $50\%$, badminton $25\%$, swimming $15\%$, other sports $10\%$.
a) $20$ pupils chose football.
b) $6$ pupils chose swimming.
c) The number choosing football is $3$ times the number choosing badminton.
d) Badminton and swimming together make $30\%$ of the pupils.
::key T T F F
a) True: $40\div100\times50=20$. b) True: $40\div100\times15=6$. c) False: $20\div10=2$, so it is $2$ times. d) False: $25\%+15\%=40\%$.

::sa
Find the percentage that $6$ is of $25$ (do not write the $\%$ sign).
::key 24
$6:25=0.24=24\%$.

::sa
A die is rolled $50$ times and the face with $6$ dots comes up $8$ times. What is the percentage of rolls that gave $6$ dots (do not write the $\%$ sign)?
::key 16
$8:50=0.16=16\%$.
:::
