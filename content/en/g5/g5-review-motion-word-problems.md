A car leaves A at $6{:}30$ and travels the $168$ km to B at $48$ km/h, stopping for a $15$-minute rest on the way. At what time does the car arrive at B?

This problem needs uniform motion and careful reading. Let's revise what you learnt in Year $5$.

## Uniform motion

Remember the three formulas: $v=s\div t$, $s=v\times t$, $t=s\div v$. The units must match: km and hours give km/h. When the time includes minutes, change it to hours: $30$ minutes $=0.5$ hour, $40$ minutes $=\dfrac23$ hour, $15$ minutes $=0.25$ hour.

:::example Finding speed and distance
A cyclist rides $15$ km in $1$ hour $15$ minutes. a) Find the speed. b) At that speed, how many kilometres does the cyclist ride in $40$ minutes?

**Solution.** a) We have $1$ h $15$ min $=1.25$ h, so
$$v=15\div1.25=12\ (\text{km/h}).$$
b) We have $40$ min $=\dfrac23$ h, so
$$s=12\times\frac23=8\ (\text{km}).$$
So the speed is $12$ km/h and in $40$ minutes the cyclist rides $8$ km.
:::

When a journey has two parts, the average speed is the total distance divided by the total time.

:::example Average speed
A vehicle goes from A to B, $120$ km, at $40$ km/h, then returns to A at $60$ km/h. Find the average speed for the whole round trip.

**Solution.** The time out is $120\div40=3$ (hours). The time back is $120\div60=2$ (hours). In all, the vehicle travels $120\times2=240$ (km) in $3+2=5$ (hours). The average speed is:
$$240\div5=48\ (\text{km/h}).$$
**Remark.** The number $48$ is not the mean of $40$ and $60$. The vehicle spends more time going slowly, so the average speed is closer to $40$.

**Answer.** $48$ km/h.
:::

## Some word problems

There are two common methods. **Finding the value of one unit:** find the value of one unit, then work out the value of many units. **Proportion:** when one quantity is multiplied by some number, the other is multiplied by the same number (direct proportion) or divided by it (inverse proportion).

:::example Finding the value of one unit
a) $6$ identical exercise books cost $72\,000$ dong. How much do $10$ such books cost?
b) For the same job, $4$ people take $6$ days. How many days do $8$ people (working at the same rate) take?

**Solution.** a) One book costs $72\,000\div6=12\,000$ (dong). $10$ books cost $12\,000\times10=120\,000$ (dong).

b) The job needs $4\times6=24$ (days of work for one person). With $8$ people it takes $24\div8=3$ (days). More people means fewer days, so these quantities are in inverse proportion.

**Answer.** a) $120\,000$ dong; b) $3$ days.
:::

:::example A discount
A school bag costs $250\,000$ dong and has a $20\%$ discount. What does the bag cost after the discount?

**Solution.** The discount is $250\,000\div100\times20=50\,000$ (dong). The price after the discount is:
$$250\,000-50\,000=200\,000\ (\text{dong}).$$
**Check.** The new price is $80\%$ of the old price, and $250\,000\div100\times80=200\,000$. Correct.

**Answer.** $200\,000$ dong.
:::

Now we answer the problem at the start, using *Understand, Plan, Solve, Look back*.

:::example The car from A to B
A car leaves A at $6{:}30$ and travels the $168$ km to B at $48$ km/h, stopping for a $15$-minute rest on the way. At what time does it arrive at B?

**Solution.**

*Understand.* The distance is $168$ km, the speed is $48$ km/h, the rest is $15$ min and the start is $6{:}30$. We need the arrival time.

*Plan.* Find the travelling time, add the rest, then add the total to the start time.

*Solve.* The travelling time is $168\div48=3.5$ (hours) $=3$ h $30$ min. The whole journey takes $3$ h $30$ min $+$ $15$ min $=3$ h $45$ min. The car arrives at:
$$6\text{ h }30\text{ min}+3\text{ h }45\text{ min}=9\text{ h }75\text{ min}=10\text{ h }15\text{ min}.$$
*Look back.* The result makes sense, since the car travels for more than $3$ hours. Without the rest, it would arrive at $10{:}00$. If it went faster, at $56$ km/h, the journey would take $168\div56=3$ hours, which is $30$ minutes less than at $48$ km/h.

**Answer.** $10{:}15$.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Average speed as the mean of two speeds.** Wrong: $(40+60)\div2=50$ km/h. Why it is wrong: the two parts take different times. Right: total distance divided by total time gives $48$ km/h.
- **Using the percentage as the new price.** Wrong: the price after a $20\%$ discount is $250\,000\div100\times20=50\,000$ dong. Why it is wrong: $50\,000$ dong is the discount. Right: $250\,000-50\,000=200\,000$ dong.
- **Mixing up inverse and direct proportion.** Wrong: $4$ people take $6$ days, so $8$ people take $12$ days. Why it is wrong: more people take fewer days. Right: $3$ days.
:::

## Summary

- $v=s\div t$; $s=v\times t$; $t=s\div v$. Change the time to hours before calculating.
- Average speed $=$ total distance $\div$ total time.
- Value of one unit: find the value of one, then multiply. Inverse proportion: the product of the two quantities stays the same.
- Discount of $p\%$: discount $=$ price $\div100\times p$; new price $=$ old price $-$ discount.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
A car travels $175$ km in $3.5$ hours. Find the speed of the car.

::hint
**What you need:** $v=s\div t$.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. Divide the distance by the time.

::solution
$v=175\div3.5=50$ (km/h).

**Answer.** $50$ km/h.
:::

:::exercise
A cyclist rides at $12$ km/h. How long does the cyclist take to ride $27$ km?

::hint
**What you need:** $t=s\div v$; $0.25$ hour $=15$ minutes.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $27\div12$.
2. Change the decimal part of the hour to minutes.

::solution
$t=27\div12=2.25$ (hours) $=2$ h $15$ min.

**Answer.** $2$ hours $15$ minutes.
:::

:::exercise
A motorbike travels at $36$ km/h. How many kilometres does it travel in $20$ minutes?

::hint
**What you need:** $20$ min $=\dfrac13$ h; $s=v\times t$.

**Steps:**
1. Change $20$ minutes to hours.
2. Multiply by the speed.

::solution
$s=36\times\dfrac13=12$ (km).

**Answer.** $12$ km.
:::

:::exercise
A bus leaves at $7{:}20$ and travels $90$ km at $45$ km/h. At what time does it arrive?

::hint
**What you need:** arrival time $=$ departure time $+$ travelling time.

**Steps:**
1. Find the travelling time.
2. Add it to the departure time.

::solution
The travelling time is $90\div45=2$ (hours). The bus arrives at $7{:}20$ plus $2$ h, which is $9{:}20$.

**Answer.** $9{:}20$.
:::

:::exercise
A vehicle travels $60$ km in $1$ hour, then another $90$ km in $2$ hours. Find its average speed over the whole distance.

::hint
**What you need:** average speed $=$ total distance $\div$ total time.

**Steps:**
1. Find the total distance and the total time.
2. Divide.
3. Check: does $50\times3$ equal the total distance?

::solution
The total distance is $60+90=150$ (km). The total time is $1+2=3$ (hours). The average speed is $150\div3=50$ (km/h).

**Answer.** $50$ km/h.
:::

:::exercise
$6$ identical exercise books cost $72\,000$ dong. How much do $15$ such books cost?

::hint
**What you need:** find the value of one unit: the price of one book.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. Find the price of one book, then multiply by $15$.

::solution
One book costs $72\,000\div6=12\,000$ (dong). $15$ books cost $12\,000\times15=180\,000$ (dong).

**Answer.** $180\,000$ dong.
:::

:::exercise
A shop gives a $15\%$ discount on a shirt that costs $200\,000$ dong. What is the price of the shirt after the discount?

::hint
**What you need:** discount $=$ price $\div100\times15$.

**Steps:**
1. Find the amount of the discount.
2. Subtract it from the old price.

::solution
The discount is $200\,000\div100\times15=30\,000$ (dong). The price after the discount is $200\,000-30\,000=170\,000$ (dong).

**Answer.** $170\,000$ dong.
:::

:::exercise
A team of $6$ workers repairs a road in $8$ days. How many workers are needed to repair it in $6$ days (each worker works at the same rate)?

::hint
**What you need:** inverse proportion: the product of the number of workers and the number of days stays the same.

**Steps:**
1. Find the days of work for the whole job.
2. Divide by the new number of days.

::solution
The job needs $6\times8=48$ (days of work). To finish in $6$ days we need $48\div6=8$ (workers).

**Answer.** $8$ workers.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the lesson on adding and subtracting times. A vehicle leaves at $8{:}45$ and arrives at $11{:}15$ at $52$ km/h (with no rest). Find the distance it travelled.

::hint
**What you need:** travelling time $=$ arrival time $-$ departure time; $s=v\times t$.

**Steps:**
1. Subtract the two times.
2. Change to hours, then multiply by the speed.

::solution
The travelling time is $11$ h $15$ min $-$ $8$ h $45$ min $=10$ h $75$ min $-$ $8$ h $45$ min $=2$ h $30$ min $=2.5$ h. The distance is $52\times2.5=130$ (km).

**Answer.** $130$ km.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
A person goes from A to B at $30$ km/h, then returns from B to A at $20$ km/h. Find the average speed for the round trip. (Try AB $=60$ km.)

::hint
**What you need:** average speed $=$ total distance $\div$ total time.

**Steps:**
1. Find the time out and the time back.
2. Divide the total distance by the total time.

::solution
The time out is $60\div30=2$ (hours) and the time back is $60\div20=3$ (hours). The total distance is $60\times2=120$ (km) and the total time is $5$ hours. The average speed is $120\div5=24$ (km/h).

**Remark.** If AB $=120$ km, the trip out takes $4$ hours and the trip back $6$ hours, and $240\div10=24$: the result does not change.

**Answer.** $24$ km/h.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
A car travels $150$ km in $2.5$ hours. Its speed is
A. $375$ km/h.
B. $6$ km/h.
C. $60$ km/h.
D. $152.5$ km/h.
::key C
$v=150\div2.5=60$ km/h. Option A multiplies the two numbers, B loses the decimal point in $2.5$ and D adds the two numbers.

::mc
A vehicle travels at $45$ km/h for $20$ minutes. The distance it travels is
A. $900$ km.
B. $2.25$ km.
C. $65$ km.
D. $15$ km.
::key D
$20$ min $=\dfrac13$ h, so $s=45\times\dfrac13=15$ km. Option A works out $45\times20$ (without changing minutes to hours), B divides $45\div20$ and C adds the two numbers.

::mc
$4$ identical pens cost $48\,000$ dong. $7$ such pens cost
A. $84\,000$ dong.
B. $192\,000$ dong.
C. $12\,000$ dong.
D. $55\,000$ dong.
::key A
One pen costs $12\,000$ dong, so $7$ pens cost $84\,000$ dong. Option B works out $48\,000\times4$, C is the price of one pen and D adds $48\,000+7000$.

::mc
A school bag costs $250\,000$ dong and has a $20\%$ discount. The price after the discount is
A. $50\,000$ dong.
B. $200\,000$ dong.
C. $230\,000$ dong.
D. $249\,980$ dong.
::key B
The discount is $50\,000$ dong, so the new price is $200\,000$ dong. Option A is the discount, C treats $20\%$ as $20\,000$ dong and D subtracts $20$ dong.

::tf
A car travels from A to B, $120$ km, at $40$ km/h, then returns to A at $60$ km/h.
a) The time from A to B is $3$ hours.
b) The time from B to A is $2$ hours.
c) The average speed for the round trip is $50$ km/h.
d) The average speed for the round trip is $48$ km/h.
::key T T F T
a) True: $120\div40=3$. b) True: $120\div60=2$. c) False: $50$ is the mean of the two speeds, not the average speed. d) True: $240\div5=48$.

::sa
A vehicle travels $90$ km at $45$ km/h. How many minutes does it take?
::key 120
$90\div45=2$ hours $=120$ minutes.

::sa
For the same job, $6$ workers take $8$ days. How many days do $12$ workers (working at the same rate) take?
::key 4
$6\times8=48$ days of work, $48\div12=4$ (days).
:::
