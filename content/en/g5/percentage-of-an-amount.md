:::goals
- Understand that $a\%$ of an amount means $a$ parts out of $100$ equal parts of it.
- Find a percentage of an amount in two ways.
- Solve word problems, for example discount problems.
:::

The school library has $200$ books. The librarian says that $35\%$ of the books are comics. How many comics does the library have?

You already know that $35\%$ means $35$ parts out of $100$ equal parts. This lesson shows you how to find the number of books.

## Let's explore

Think of all $200$ books as $100\%$ and split them into $100$ equal parts.

- One part is $1\%$: $200\div100=2$ (books).
- $35\%$ is $35$ such parts: $2\times35=70$ (books).

So $35\%$ of $200$ is $70$. The library has $70$ comics.

:::theorem Finding a percentage of an amount
To find $a\%$ of an amount $B$, use one of two methods:
1. Divide $B$ by $100$, then multiply by $a$: $B\div100\times a$.
2. Multiply $B$ by $a$, then divide by $100$: $B\times a\div100$.
:::

Both methods work because $1\%$ of $B$ is $B\div100$, and $a\%$ is $a$ such parts. Multiplying by $a$ first or dividing by $100$ first gives the same result. Choose the method that makes the calculation easier.

## Examples

We start with $250$, because dividing it by $100$ gives a tidy decimal.

:::example Work out $40\%$ of $250$
**Solution.** Use method 1: divide $250$ by $100$ to find $1\%$, then multiply by $40$:
$$250\div100\times40=2.5\times40=100.$$
So $40\%$ of $250$ is $100$.
:::

With $360$, it is better to multiply first and avoid decimals.

:::example Work out $15\%$ of $360$
**Solution.** Use method 2: multiply $360$ by $15$, then divide by $100$ (remove two zeros at the end):
$$360\times15\div100=5\,400\div100=54.$$
So $15\%$ of $360$ is $54$.
:::

Some percentages are so familiar that you can do them in your head.

:::example Work out $50\%$ of $86$ and $25\%$ of $120$ in your head
**Solution.** We have $50\%=\dfrac{50}{100}=\dfrac12$ and $25\%=\dfrac{25}{100}=\dfrac14$. Finding a half means dividing by $2$, and finding a quarter means dividing by $4$:
$$86\div2=43,\qquad 120\div4=30.$$
So $50\%$ of $86$ is $43$ and $25\%$ of $120$ is $30$.

**Check.** $120\times25\div100=3\,000\div100=30$. Correct.
:::

Now use the method to solve a discount problem.

:::example A discount on a school bag
A school bag costs $250\,000$ dong. The shop gives a $20\%$ discount. How much do you pay?

**Solution.** The discount is $20\%$ of the price of the bag, so find $20\%$ of $250\,000$:
$$250\,000\times20\div100=5\,000\,000\div100=50\,000\ \text{(dong)}.$$
The amount you pay is the original price minus the discount:
$$250\,000-50\,000=200\,000\ \text{(dong)}.$$
So you pay $200\,000$ dong.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Forgetting to divide by $100$.** Wrong: $20\%$ of $150$ is $150\times20=3\,000$. Why it is wrong: $3\,000$ is far bigger than $150$, but a part of $150$ cannot be bigger than $150$. Right: $150\times20\div100=30$.
- **Dividing by the percentage.** Wrong: $20\%$ of $150$ is $150\div20=7.5$. Why it is wrong: the whole amount is $100\%$, so you divide by $100$, not by $20$. Right: $150\div100\times20=30$.
- **Writing $\%$ in the answer.** Wrong: "$20\%$ of $150$ is $30\%$". Why it is wrong: the result is a number of books, a sum of money and so on, not a percentage. Right: $30$ (with its unit).
- **Forgetting to subtract in a discount problem.** Wrong: the price to pay is $50\,000$ dong. Why it is wrong: $50\,000$ dong is the discount. Right: $250\,000-50\,000=200\,000$ dong.
:::

## Summary

- $a\%$ of $B$ is $a$ parts out of $100$ equal parts of $B$.
- Method 1: $B\div100\times a$. Method 2: $B\times a\div100$. Both give the same result.
- Quick facts: $50\%$ is a half (divide by $2$) and $25\%$ is a quarter (divide by $4$).
- Discount problems: the discount is a percentage of the original price; the amount to pay is the original price minus the discount.
- The result is a number with a unit, so do not write $\%$ in it.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
Work out $20\%$ of $350$.

::hint
**What you need:** $a\%$ of $B$ is $B\div100\times a$.

**Steps:**
1. Divide $350$ by $100$ to find $1\%$.
2. Multiply the result by $20$.

::solution
$350\div100\times20=3.5\times20=70$.

**Answer.** $70$.
:::

:::exercise
Work out $5\%$ of $420$.

::hint
**What you need:** $a\%$ of $B$ is $B\times a\div100$.

**Steps:**
1. Multiply $420$ by $5$.
2. Divide the result by $100$.

::solution
$420\times5\div100=2\,100\div100=21$.

**Answer.** $21$.
:::

:::exercise
Work out $50\%$ and $25\%$ of $64$ in your head.

::hint
**What you need:** $50\%=\dfrac12$ and $25\%=\dfrac14$.

**Steps:**
1. Find a half of $64$: divide by $2$.
2. Find a quarter of $64$: divide by $4$.

::solution
$64\div2=32$ and $64\div4=16$.

**Answer.** $50\%$ of $64$ is $32$; $25\%$ of $64$ is $16$.
:::

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$ in the box: $30\%$ of $200\;\square\;40\%$ of $160$.

::hint
**What you need:** work out each percentage first, then compare the two numbers.

**Steps:**
1. Find $30\%$ of $200$.
2. Find $40\%$ of $160$, then compare.

::solution
$200\times30\div100=60$ and $160\times40\div100=6\,400\div100=64$. Since $60<64$, $30\%$ of $200$ is less than $40\%$ of $160$.

**Answer.** The sign $<$.
:::

:::exercise
Lan reads a book with $180$ pages. On the first day she reads $40\%$ of the pages. How many pages does she read on the first day, and how many pages are left?

::hint
**What you need:** the pages read on day one are $40\%$ of $180$; the pages left are the total minus the pages read.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What is asked? The book has $180$ pages; we need the pages read and the pages left.
2. Find $40\%$ of $180$, then subtract the result from $180$.
3. Check: add the two numbers you found and see whether you get $180$.

::solution
The number of pages Lan reads on the first day is:
$$180\times40\div100=7\,200\div100=72\ \text{(pages)}.$$
The number of pages left is $180-72=108$ (pages).

**Check.** $72+108=180$. Correct.

**Answer.** $72$ pages; $108$ pages are left.
:::

:::exercise
A pair of shoes costs $400\,000$ dong. The shop gives a $15\%$ discount. How much do you pay?

::hint
**What you need:** the discount is $15\%$ of the original price; the amount to pay is the original price minus the discount.

**Steps:**
1. Find $15\%$ of $400\,000$.
2. Subtract the discount from $400\,000$.

::solution
The discount is:
$$400\,000\times15\div100=6\,000\,000\div100=60\,000\ \text{(dong)}.$$
The amount to pay is $400\,000-60\,000=340\,000$ (dong).

**Answer.** $340\,000$ dong.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the last lesson. Class 5C has $40$ pupils, and $14$ of them are boys. Find the boys as a percentage of the whole class. Then use today's lesson to check: find that percentage of $40$.

::hint
**What you need:** to write $a$ as a percentage of $b$, divide $a$ by $b$, then write the quotient as a percentage.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $14\div40$ and change it to a percentage.
2. Find that percentage of $40$ and compare it with $14$.

::solution
$14\div40=0.35=35\%$.

**Check.** $40\times35\div100=1\,400\div100=14$, which is exactly the number of boys.

**Answer.** $35\%$.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
A rectangular garden is $40$ m long, and its width is $60\%$ of its length. Then $25\%$ of the area of the garden is used to grow vegetables. Find the area used for vegetables.

::hint
**What you need:** the area of a rectangle is length times width.

**Steps:**
1. Find the width: $60\%$ of $40$ m.
2. Work out the area of the garden.
3. Find $25\%$ of that area.

::solution
The width is $40\times60\div100=24$ (m). The area of the garden is $40\times24=960$ ($\text{m}^2$). The area for vegetables is $25\%$ of $960$, which is a quarter of $960$:
$$960\div4=240\ (\text{m}^2).$$

**Check.** $960\times25\div100=24\,000\div100=240$. Correct.

**Answer.** $240\ \text{m}^2$.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
$20\%$ of $150$ is
A. $3\,000$.
B. $7.5$.
C. $30$.
D. $750$.
::key C
$150\times20\div100=3\,000\div100=30$. Option A is the mistake of forgetting to divide by $100$; option B is $150\div20$.

::mc
To find $12\%$ of $350$, you work out
A. $350\div12$.
B. $350\times100\div12$.
C. $350\div100\div12$.
D. $350\times12\div100$.
::key D
$12\%$ of $350$ is $12$ parts out of $100$ parts of $350$, that is $350\times12\div100$. The other options divide by $12$, which is wrong.

::mc
A shirt costs $300\,000$ dong and has a $10\%$ discount. The amount to pay is
A. $270\,000$ dong.
B. $330\,000$ dong.
C. $3\,000\,000$ dong.
D. $30\,000$ dong.
::key A
The discount is $300\,000\times10\div100=30\,000$ dong, so you pay $300\,000-30\,000=270\,000$ dong. Option D forgets to subtract.

::mc
A class has $30$ pupils, and $40\%$ of them walk to school. The number of pupils who walk is
A. $1\,200$ pupils.
B. $12$ pupils.
C. $75$ pupils.
D. $70$ pupils.
::key B
$30\times40\div100=1\,200\div100=12$ (pupils). Option A forgets to divide by $100$; option C is $30\div40\times100$, which finds a percentage, not a percentage of an amount.

::tf
Look at $60\%$ of $90$.
a) It can be worked out as $90\div100\times60$.
b) The result is $54$.
c) The result is $5\,400$.
d) The result equals $40\%$ of $140$.
::key T T F F
a) True: divide by $100$, then multiply by $60$. b) True: $0.9\times60=54$. c) False: $5\,400$ is $90\times60$, not yet divided by $100$. d) False: $140\times40\div100=56$, not $54$.

::sa
Work out $35\%$ of $80$.
::key 28
$80\times35\div100=2\,800\div100=28$.

::sa
A shop has $450$ kg of rice and sells $60\%$ of it. How many kilograms of rice are left?
::key 180
The rice sold is $450\times60\div100=270$ (kg), so $450-270=180$ (kg) are left.
:::
