You have almost finished primary school. This paper has $10$ questions, each revisiting a type of problem from Grade $5$, worth $10$ marks in total. Spend $40$ minutes on it, then open the solutions and mark yourself. How many marks will you score?

## How to tackle a mixed paper

Read the whole paper once and do the easy questions first. For every word problem, ask yourself: what is given, what is asked, which operation is needed? When you finish, check: is the answer sensible, and have you written the unit?

A quick reminder of what you will need:
- Decimals: line up the decimal points when adding or subtracting. Fractions: use a common denominator when adding or subtracting.
- Time: $60$ minutes $=1$ hour. Uniform motion: $v=s\div t$, $s=v\times t$, $t=s\div v$.
- Volume: cuboid $a\times b\times c$; cube $a\times a\times a$; $1\ \text{dm}^3=1$ litre.
- Area of a trapezium: $(a+b)\times h\div2$. Percentages: $p\%$ of $A$ is $A\div100\times p$.

The next three examples show how to set out your work.

:::example Calculating cleverly
Calculate $2.5\times4.7\times4$.

**Solution.** Since $2.5\times4=10$ is a round number, we use the commutative and associative properties:
$$2.5\times4.7\times4=(2.5\times4)\times4.7=10\times4.7=47.$$
So $2.5\times4.7\times4=47$.
:::

:::example Water in a tank
A cuboid tank is $6$ dm long, $4$ dm wide and $5$ dm high. It holds water equal to $\dfrac34$ of its volume. How many litres of water are in the tank?

**Solution.** The volume of the tank is $6\times4\times5=120\ (\text{dm}^3)$. The amount of water is:
$$120\times\frac34=90\ (\text{dm}^3)=90\ \text{litres}.$$
**Answer.** $90$ litres.
:::

The next problem compares two journeys, so we go step by step: *Understand, Plan, Solve, Look back*.

:::example Who cycles faster?
An cycles $10$ km in $50$ minutes. Binh cycles $9$ km in $40$ minutes. Who is faster, and by how many kilometres per hour?

**Solution.**

*Understand.* An: $10$ km in $50$ minutes. Binh: $9$ km in $40$ minutes. We must find who is faster and by how many km/h.

*Plan.* Work out each person's speed in km/h, then compare.

*Solve.* $50$ minutes $=\dfrac56$ hour and $40$ minutes $=\dfrac23$ hour. An's speed is $10\div\dfrac56=12$ (km/h). Binh's speed is $9\div\dfrac23=13.5$ (km/h). Since $13.5>12$, Binh is faster, by $13.5-12=1.5$ (km/h).

*Look back.* Try another way: in $10$ minutes, An travels $10\div5=2$ km while Binh travels $9\div4=2.25$ km, so Binh is faster. Both ways give the same conclusion.

**Answer.** Binh is faster, by $1.5$ km/h.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to avoid
- **Forgetting to change minutes to hours.** Wrong: at $15$ km/h for $40$ minutes, $s=15\times40=600$ km. Why it is wrong: $40$ minutes is $\dfrac23$ hour. Right: $15\times\dfrac23=10$ km.
- **Subtracting numerators and denominators.** Wrong: $\dfrac56-\dfrac14=\dfrac42$. Why it is wrong: fractions with different denominators need a common denominator first. Right: $\dfrac{10}{12}-\dfrac3{12}=\dfrac7{12}$.
- **Using the percentage as the answer.** Wrong: $25\%$ of $80$ is $25$. Why it is wrong: $25\%$ means $25$ parts out of $100$. Right: $80\div100\times25=20$.
:::

## Practice

Each question has a reminder and a hint. Try it yourself first, then open the solution. The marks for each question are in brackets.

:::exercise
**Question 1. (0.5 marks)** Evaluate $(3.2+1.8)\times4-6\div2$.

::hint
**What you need:** brackets first; multiplication and division before addition and subtraction.

**Steps:**
1. Work out the brackets.
2. Calculate $5\times4$ and $6\div2$, then subtract.

::solution
$$(3.2+1.8)\times4-6\div2=5\times4-3=20-3=17.$$
**Answer.** $17$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 2. (1.0 mark)** Calculate $\dfrac56-\dfrac14+\dfrac13$.

::hint
**What you need:** use a common denominator, then add and subtract the numerators.

**Steps:**
1. The lowest common denominator of $6$, $4$, $3$ is $12$.
2. Work from left to right.

::solution
$$\frac56-\frac14+\frac13=\frac{10}{12}-\frac3{12}+\frac4{12}=\frac{11}{12}.$$
**Answer.** $\dfrac{11}{12}$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 3. (1.0 mark)** Set out in columns and calculate: a) $25.4-8.75$; b) $3.6\times2.5$.

::hint
**What you need:** line up the decimal points to add or subtract; to multiply, count the decimal places in the two factors.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: write $25.4=25.40$.
2. Part b: multiply $36\times25$, then place the decimal point.

::solution
a) $25.40-8.75=16.65$.

b) $36\times25=900$ and the product has two decimal places, so $3.6\times2.5=9.00=9$.

**Answer.** a) $16.65$; b) $9$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 4. (1.0 mark)** A cube-shaped fish tank has edges of $5$ dm. Water is poured in until it fills $\dfrac45$ of the tank. How many litres of water are in the tank?

::hint
**What you need:** $V=a\times a\times a$; $1\ \text{dm}^3=1$ litre.

**Steps:**
1. What is given? What is asked?
2. Find the volume of the tank.
3. Take $\dfrac45$ of the volume.

::solution
The volume of the tank is $5\times5\times5=125\ (\text{dm}^3)$. The water is $125\times\dfrac45=100\ (\text{dm}^3)=100$ litres.

**Answer.** $100$ litres.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 5. (1.0 mark)** An extra class starts at $14{:}40$. It has $3$ lessons of $40$ minutes each, with $2$ breaks of $10$ minutes each. At what time does the class finish?

::hint
**What you need:** multiply a time by a number; add times.

**Steps:**
1. What is given? What is asked?
2. Find the total time for lessons and breaks.
3. Add it to the start time.

::solution
Lesson time is $40\times3=120$ (min). Break time is $10\times2=20$ (min). The whole class lasts $120+20=140$ (min) $=2$ h $20$ min. The class finishes at:
$$14\text{ h }40\text{ min}+2\text{ h }20\text{ min}=16\text{ h }60\text{ min}=17\text{ h}.$$
**Answer.** $17{:}00$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 6. (1.0 mark)** A motorbike travels at $54$ km/h for $2$ hours $20$ minutes. a) Find the distance it travels. b) If it sets off at $6{:}45$, at what time does it arrive?

::hint
**What you need:** $s=v\times t$; $20$ minutes $=\dfrac13$ hour.

**Steps:**
1. Change $2$ h $20$ min into hours.
2. Multiply by the speed.
3. Add the travel time to the start time.

::solution
a) $2$ h $20$ min $=\dfrac73$ h. The distance is $54\times\dfrac73=126$ (km).

b) It arrives at $6$ h $45$ min $+$ $2$ h $20$ min $=8$ h $65$ min $=9$ h $5$ min.

**Answer.** a) $126$ km; b) $9{:}05$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 7. (1.0 mark)** A pie chart shows the clubs chosen by $80$ students: Chess $25\%$, Art $35\%$, Table tennis $15\%$, and the rest chose Singing. How many more students are in the Art club than in the Table tennis club?

::hint
**What you need:** the whole circle is $100\%$, which stands for $80$ students.

**Steps:**
1. Find the number of students in Art.
2. Find the number in Table tennis, then subtract.

::solution
Art has $80\div100\times35=28$ students. Table tennis has $80\div100\times15=12$ students. The difference is $28-12=16$ students.

**Check.** Chess has $20$ students; Singing is $100-(25+35+15)=25\ (\%)$, so $20$ students; $20+28+12+20=80$.

**Answer.** $16$ students.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 8. (1.0 mark)** A field is a trapezium with parallel sides of $24$ m and $16$ m. Its height is half of the shorter parallel side. Find the area of the field.

::hint
**What you need:** $S=(a+b)\times h\div2$.

**Steps:**
1. Find the height.
2. Use the formula.

::solution
The height is $16\div2=8$ (m). The area is $(24+16)\times8\div2=160\ (\text{m}^2)$.

**Answer.** $160\ \text{m}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
**Question 9. (1.0 mark)** Ha tosses a coin $80$ times and gets tails $36$ times. Find the percentage of tosses that were tails and the percentage that were heads.

::hint
**What you need:** ratio $=$ number of times the event happens $\div$ total number of tosses.

**Steps:**
1. Find the number of heads.
2. Work out both ratios, then add them as a check.

::solution
Tails: $36\div80=0.45=45\%$. Heads came up $80-36=44$ times: $44\div80=0.55=55\%$.

**Answer.** Tails $45\%$; heads $55\%$.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
**Question 10. (1.5 marks)** Suppose Mr Nam puts $50$ million dong in a savings account at an interest rate of $6\%$ per year. After one year he withdraws the money and the interest, and spends $\dfrac25$ of the total on a bicycle for his child. a) Find the interest. b) Find the cost of the bicycle. c) Find the money left.

::hint
**What you need:** interest $=$ deposit $\div100\times6$; fraction of an amount.

**Steps:**
1. What is given? What is asked?
2. Find the interest, then the deposit plus interest.
3. Find $\dfrac25$ of that total, then the money left.

::solution
a) The interest is $50\div100\times6=3$ (million dong).

b) The total is $50+3=53$ (million dong). The bicycle costs $53\times\dfrac25=21.2$ (million dong).

c) The money left is $53-21.2=31.8$ (million dong).

**Answer.** a) $3$ million dong; b) $21.2$ million dong; c) $31.8$ million dong.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
The value of $3.6\times2.5$ is
A. $90$.
B. $6.1$.
C. $0.9$.
D. $9$.
::key D
$36\times25=900$ and the product has two decimal places, so $3.6\times2.5=9$. Option A forgets the decimal point, C misplaces it, B adds instead.

::mc
The volume of a cube with edge $5$ dm is
A. $125\ \text{dm}^3$.
B. $25\ \text{dm}^3$.
C. $125\ \text{dm}^2$.
D. $15\ \text{dm}^3$.
::key A
$5\times5\times5=125\ (\text{dm}^3)$. Option D is $5\times3$, B is $5\times5$, C has the wrong unit.

::mc
A motorbike travels at $54$ km/h for $2$ hours $20$ minutes. The distance travelled is
A. $124.2$ km.
B. $126$ km.
C. $108$ km.
D. $118.8$ km.
::key B
$2$ h $20$ min $=\dfrac73$ h, so $s=54\times\dfrac73=126$ km. Option A treats $20$ minutes as $0.3$ h, C ignores the $20$ minutes, D treats $2$ h $20$ min as $2.2$ h.

::mc
$25\%$ of $80$ is
A. $25$.
B. $320$.
C. $20$.
D. $2000$.
::key C
$80\div100\times25=20$. Option A takes the percentage itself, B is $80\div0.25$, D multiplies $80\times25$ without dividing by $100$.

::tf
A coin is tossed $80$ times and lands tails $36$ times.
a) The percentage of tails is $45\%$.
b) Heads came up $44$ times.
c) The percentage of heads is $55\%$.
d) The percentage of tails is greater than the percentage of heads.
::key T T T F
a) True: $36\div80=0.45$. b) True: $80-36=44$. c) True: $44\div80=0.55$. d) False: $45\%<55\%$.

::sa
A trapezium has parallel sides $24$ m and $16$ m and height $8$ m. What is its area in square metres?
::key 160
$(24+16)\times8\div2=160$.

::sa
$6$ identical exercise books cost $72\,000$ dong. What is the price of one book, in thousands of dong?
::key 12
$72\,000\div6=12\,000$ dong $=12$ thousand dong.
:::
