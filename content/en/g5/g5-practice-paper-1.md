## Part 1. Multiple choice (4 marks)

:::exercise
The digit $7$ in the number $35.172$ has the value $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $7$.
B. $70$.
C. $\dfrac{7}{10}$.
D. $\dfrac{7}{100}$.

::hint
**What you need:** after the decimal point the places are tenths, hundredths, thousandths.

**Steps:**
1. Find which place after the decimal point the digit $7$ is in.
2. Write the value of that place as a fraction.

::solution
After the decimal point, the digit $1$ is in the tenths place, the digit $7$ is in the hundredths place and the digit $2$ is in the thousandths place. So the digit $7$ has the value $\dfrac{7}{100}=0.07$.

**Answer.** D. Revise: [Review: decimals and percentages](lesson:g5-review-decimals-percent).
:::

:::exercise
The result of $\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{1}{6}$ is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $\dfrac{2}{10}$.
B. $\dfrac{7}{12}$.
C. $\dfrac{11}{12}$.
D. $\dfrac{2}{4}$.

::hint
**What you need:** to subtract fractions with different denominators, write them over a common denominator, then subtract the numerators.

**Steps:**
1. Find a common denominator of $4$ and $6$.
2. Rewrite the fractions, then subtract.

::solution
A common denominator is $12$:
$$\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{9}{12}-\dfrac{2}{12}=\dfrac{7}{12}.$$

**Answer.** B. Revise: Review: operations with fractions.
:::

:::exercise
A class has $40$ pupils, of whom $18$ are girls. The percentage of the class who are girls is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $18\%$.
B. $45\%$.
C. $55\%$.
D. $222\%$.

::hint
**What you need:** to find $a$ as a percentage of $b$, work out $a\div b$ and multiply by $100$.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $18\div 40$.
2. Multiply by $100$ and write the sign $\%$.

::solution
$18\div 40=0.45=45\%$.

**Answer.** B. Revise: [Review: decimals and percentages](lesson:g5-review-decimals-percent).
:::

:::exercise
A circular plate has radius $5\ \text{cm}$. Take $\pi=3.14$. The circumference of the plate is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $15.7\ \text{cm}$.
B. $31.4\ \text{cm}$.
C. $62.8\ \text{cm}$.
D. $78.5\ \text{cm}$.

::hint
**What you need:** the circumference of a circle is the radius times $2$ times $3.14$.

**Steps:**
1. Recall the formula for the circumference.
2. Put in the radius $5\ \text{cm}$.

::solution
The circumference of the plate is:
$$5\times 2\times 3.14=31.4\ \text{(cm)}.$$

**Answer.** B. Revise: Review: geometry and measures.
:::

:::exercise
A water tank is a cuboid $2\ \text{m}$ long, $1.5\ \text{m}$ wide and $1\ \text{m}$ high. How many litres of water does it hold when full? $(0.5$ marks$)$
A. $3$ litres.
B. $30$ litres.
C. $300$ litres.
D. $3\,000$ litres.

::hint
**What you need:** the volume of a cuboid is length times width times height; $1\ \text{m}^3=1\,000\ \text{dm}^3=1\,000$ litres.

**Steps:**
1. Work out the volume of the tank in $\text{m}^3$.
2. Convert it to litres.

::solution
The volume of the tank is $2\times 1.5\times 1=3\ (\text{m}^3)$. Since $1\ \text{m}^3=1\,000$ litres, the tank holds $3\times 1\,000=3\,000$ litres.

**Answer.** D. Revise: Review: geometry and measures.
:::

:::exercise
A motorbike travels $90\ \text{km}$ in $2$ hours $30$ minutes. The speed of the motorbike is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $30\ \text{km/h}$.
B. $36\ \text{km/h}$.
C. $45\ \text{km/h}$.
D. $225\ \text{km/h}$.

::hint
**What you need:** speed equals distance divided by time.

**Steps:**
1. Write $2$ hours $30$ minutes as hours (a decimal).
2. Divide the distance by the time.

::solution
$2$ hours $30$ minutes $=2.5$ hours. The speed of the motorbike is:
$$90\div 2.5=36\ \text{(km/h)}.$$

**Answer.** B. Revise: Review: uniform motion and word problems.
:::

:::exercise
The value of the expression $125-3\times(18+12)\div 5$ is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $107$.
B. $732$.
C. $7$.
D. $18$.

::hint
**What you need:** brackets first; then multiplication and division from left to right; addition and subtraction last.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $18+12$.
2. Work out $3\times 30$, then divide by $5$.
3. Subtract the result from $125$.

::solution
Work in this order:
$$\begin{aligned}125-3\times(18+12)\div 5&=125-3\times 30\div 5\\&=125-90\div 5\\&=125-18=107.\end{aligned}$$

**Answer.** A. Revise: [Review: operations with natural numbers](lesson:review-natural-number-operations).
:::

:::exercise
A plot of land is a trapezium with parallel sides $14\ \text{m}$ and $10\ \text{m}$ and height $6\ \text{m}$. The area of the plot is $(0.5$ marks$)$:
A. $144\ \text{m}^2$.
B. $72\ \text{m}^2$.
C. $84\ \text{m}^2$.
D. $24\ \text{m}^2$.

::hint
**What you need:** the area of a trapezium is the sum of the parallel sides times the height, divided by $2$.

**Steps:**
1. Add the two parallel sides.
2. Multiply by the height, then divide by $2$.

::solution
The area of the plot is:
$$(14+10)\times 6\div 2=24\times 6\div 2=72\ (\text{m}^2).$$

**Answer.** B. Revise: Review: geometry and measures.
:::

## Part 2. Written answers (6 marks)

:::exercise
**(1.5 marks)** a) Work out $48.6\div 1.8+2.75\times 4$. $(0.75$ marks$)$

b) A shop marks a shirt at $250\,000$ dong and takes $12\%$ off. How much does Nam pay for the shirt? $(0.75$ marks$)$

::hint
**What you need:** do multiplication and division before addition. To find $12\%$ of a number, multiply it by $12$ and divide by $100$.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: work out $48.6\div 1.8$ and $2.75\times 4$, then add.
2. Part b: find the amount taken off, then subtract it from the marked price.

::solution
a) $48.6\div 1.8=27$ and $2.75\times 4=11$, so $27+11=38$.

b) The amount taken off is $250\,000\times 12\div 100=30\,000$ (dong). Nam pays $250\,000-30\,000=220\,000$ (dong).

**Answer.** a) $38$. b) $220\,000$ dong. Revise: [Review: decimals and percentages](lesson:g5-review-decimals-percent).
:::

:::exercise
**(1.5 marks)** The distance $AB$ is $120\ \text{km}$. At $7{:}30$, a car leaves $A$ for $B$ at $48\ \text{km/h}$. At the same time, a motorbike leaves $B$ for $A$ at $32\ \text{km/h}$. Find:

a) the time when the two vehicles meet. $(1$ mark$)$

b) how far from $A$ they meet. $(0.5$ marks$)$

::hint
**What you need:** two objects moving towards each other from the same moment meet after the distance divided by the sum of their speeds.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What is asked? Work out the sum of the two speeds.
2. Find the time until they meet, then add it to the starting time.
3. Find the distance the car has travelled, then check.

::solution
a) Each hour the two vehicles get closer by $48+32=80\ (\text{km})$. The time until they meet is $120\div 80=1.5$ hours, that is $1$ hour $30$ minutes. They meet at $7{:}30+1$ hour $30$ minutes $=9{:}00$.

b) They meet at the distance the car has travelled from $A$: $48\times 1.5=72\ (\text{km})$.

**Check.** The motorbike travels $32\times 1.5=48\ (\text{km})$ and $72+48=120\ (\text{km})$. Correct.

**Answer.** a) $9{:}00$. b) $72\ \text{km}$. Revise: Review: uniform motion and word problems.
:::

:::exercise
**(1.5 marks)** A small swimming pool is a cuboid $6\ \text{m}$ long, $4\ \text{m}$ wide and $1.5\ \text{m}$ high.

a) The four sides and the floor of the pool (no lid) are tiled. Find the area to be tiled. $(0.75$ marks$)$

b) The water is now $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the height of the pool. How many cubic metres of water are in the pool? $(0.75$ marks$)$

::hint
**What you need:** the area of the four sides is the perimeter of the base times the height; the volume is the area of the base times the height.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: find the area of the four sides and the area of the floor, then add.
2. Part b: find the depth of the water ($\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $1.5\ \text{m}$), then the volume.

::solution
a) Area of the four sides: $(6+4)\times 2\times 1.5=30\ (\text{m}^2)$. Area of the floor: $6\times 4=24\ (\text{m}^2)$. The area to be tiled is $30+24=54\ (\text{m}^2)$.

b) Depth of the water: $1.5\times\dfrac{2}{3}=1\ (\text{m})$. Volume of water: $6\times 4\times 1=24\ (\text{m}^3)$.

**Answer.** a) $54\ \text{m}^2$. b) $24\ \text{m}^3$. Revise: Review: geometry and measures.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
**(1.5 marks)** A rectangular garden has perimeter $96\ \text{m}$. Its width is $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of its length.

a) Find the length and the width of the garden. $(1$ mark$)$

b) $35\%$ of the area of the garden is used for growing vegetables. Find the area used for vegetables. $(0.5$ marks$)$

::hint
**What you need:** half the perimeter is the length plus the width. For a sum–ratio problem, draw a bar diagram and find the value of one part.

**Steps:**
1. Work out half the perimeter.
2. The width is $3$ parts and the length is $5$ parts: find the total number of parts and the value of one part.
3. Find the area of the garden, then $35\%$ of it.

::solution
a) Half the perimeter is $96\div 2=48\ (\text{m})$, which is the length plus the width. The width is $3$ parts and the length is $5$ parts, so there are $3+5=8$ parts in all. One part is $48\div 8=6\ (\text{m})$. The width is $6\times 3=18\ (\text{m})$ and the length is $6\times 5=30\ (\text{m})$.

b) The area of the garden is $30\times 18=540\ (\text{m}^2)$. The area for vegetables is $540\times 35\div 100=189\ (\text{m}^2)$.

**Answer.** a) Length $30\ \text{m}$, width $18\ \text{m}$. b) $189\ \text{m}^2$. Revise: Review: operations with fractions.
:::
