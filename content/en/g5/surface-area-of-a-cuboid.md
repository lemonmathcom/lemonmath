A cuboid gift box is $8$ cm long, $5$ cm wide and $4$ cm high. Minh wants to put paper around its four side faces, then wrap the whole closed box. How many square centimetres of paper does each job need?

The four side faces are two rectangles measuring $8$ cm by $4$ cm and two measuring $5$ cm by $4$ cm. Their total area is
$$2\times(8\times4)+2\times(5\times4)=104\ \text{cm}^2.$$

The base perimeter is $(8+5)\times2=26$ cm. Open the four side faces into a strip $4$ cm wide; its area is also $26\times4=104\ \text{cm}^2$.

The two bases have total area $2\times8\times5=80\ \text{cm}^2$. Therefore, the area of all six faces is $104+80=184\ \text{cm}^2$.

:::theorem Surface area rules
- The **lateral surface area** is the total area of the four side faces:
$$\text{lateral surface area}=(\text{length}+\text{width})\times2\times\text{height}.$$
- The **total surface area** is the total area of all six faces:
$$\text{total surface area}=\text{lateral surface area}+2\times\text{length}\times\text{width}.$$
:::

The rule works because the side strip is as long as the base perimeter and as wide as the height. Total surface area also includes the two equal bases.

## Examples

Start with the four side faces.

:::example Find the lateral surface area
Find the lateral surface area of a cuboid $12$ cm long, $7$ cm wide and $5$ cm high.

**Solution.** Multiply the base perimeter by the height:
$$ (12+7)\times2\times5=190\ \text{cm}^2.$$
So the answer is $190\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

Now include all six faces.

:::example Find the total surface area
Find the total surface area of a cuboid $9$ dm long, $4$ dm wide and $6$ dm high.

**Solution.** The lateral area is $156\ \text{dm}^2$ and the two bases have area $72\ \text{dm}^2$:
$$156+72=228\ \text{dm}^2.$$

So the answer is $228\ \text{dm}^2$.
:::

You can also find a missing dimension.

:::example Find the height
A cuboid is $10$ cm long and $6$ cm wide. Its lateral area is $256\ \text{cm}^2$. Find its height.

**Solution.** The base perimeter is $32$ cm, so the height is
$$256\div32=8\ \text{(cm)}.$$
So the height is $8$ cm.
:::

Finally, include only the painted faces.

:::example Painting a wooden box
A box is $60$ cm long, $40$ cm wide and $30$ cm high. Its sides and top are painted. Find the painted area.

**Solution.** Add the four side faces and the top:
$$ (60+40)\times2\times30+60\times40=8\,400\ \text{cm}^2.$$
So the answer is $8\,400\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Using base area instead of base perimeter.** Wrong: $8\times5\times4$. Right: $(8+5)\times2\times4$.
- **Adding one base.** Total surface area must include **two** bases.
- **Using units wrongly.** Convert every dimension to one unit and give the answer in square units, such as $\text{cm}^2$.
:::

## Summary

- The lateral surface area is the total area of the four side faces.
- Lateral surface area $=$ perimeter of the base $\times$ height.
- Total surface area $=$ lateral surface area $+$ area of the two bases.
- Use one unit, give a square unit and include only the faces named.

## Practice

Each exercise gives you the rule and some steps. Try it yourself before opening the solution.

:::exercise
A cuboid is $11$ cm long, $4$ cm wide and $6$ cm high. Find its lateral area.

::hint
**What you need:** Base perimeter $\times$ height.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $(11+4)\times2$.
2. Multiply by $6$; use a square unit.

::solution
$$(11+4)\times2\times6=180\ \text{cm}^2.$$

**Answer.** $180\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
A cuboid is $7$ dm long, $5$ dm wide and $3$ dm high. Find its total area.

::hint
**What you need:** Lateral area $+$ two base areas.

**Steps:**
1. Find the four side faces.
2. Add both bases; check all six faces.

::solution
The total surface area is
$$ (7+5)\times2\times3+2\times7\times5=142\ \text{dm}^2.$$

**Answer.** $142\ \text{dm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
A cuboid is $8$ cm long and $4$ cm wide. Its lateral area is $216\ \text{cm}^2$. Find its height.

::hint
**What you need:** Height $=$ lateral area $\div$ base perimeter.

**Steps:**
1. Find the base perimeter.
2. Divide $216$ by it.

::solution
The base perimeter is $(8+4)\times2=24$ cm, so the height is $216\div24=9$ cm.

**Answer.** $9$ cm.
:::

:::exercise
Box A measures $10$ cm by $6$ cm by $4$ cm; B measures $8$ cm by $7$ cm by $4$ cm. Which has the greater lateral area, and by how much?

::hint
**What you need:** Find both lateral areas and compare.

**Steps:**
1. Find each area.
2. Subtract the smaller from the larger.

::solution
The two areas are $128\ \text{cm}^2$ and $120\ \text{cm}^2$, because
$$ (10+6)\times2\times4=128,\qquad(8+7)\times2\times4=120.$$

**Answer.** Box A, by $8\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
Think back: a net has two $9$ cm by $5$ cm faces, two $9$ cm by $3$ cm faces and two $5$ cm by $3$ cm faces. Find the total area.

::hint
**What you need:** A net contains all six faces.

**Steps:**
1. Find each pair's area.
2. Add the three results.

::solution
The total surface area is
$$2\times9\times5+2\times9\times3+2\times5\times3=174\ \text{cm}^2.$$

**Answer.** $174\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
A tea box is $14$ cm long, $8$ cm wide and $20$ cm high. A label covers its sides with no overlap. Find its area.

::hint
**What you need:** The label has the lateral area.

**Steps:**
1. Find the base perimeter.
2. Multiply by the height; omit the bases.

::solution
The area of the label is
$$ (14+8)\times2\times20=880\ \text{cm}^2.$$

**Answer.** $880\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise
A box is $50$ cm long, $30$ cm wide and $20$ cm high. Its sides and top are painted. Find the painted area.

::hint
**What you need:** Four side faces $+$ one base.

**Steps:**
1. Find the four side faces.
2. Add $50\times30$ for the top.

::solution
The painted area is
$$ (50+30)\times2\times20+50\times30=4\,700\ \text{cm}^2.$$

**Answer.** $4\,700\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
A cuboid is $10$ cm long and $4$ cm wide. Its total area is $248\ \text{cm}^2$. Find its height.

::hint
**What you need:** Total area minus the two bases.

**Steps:**
1. Find the lateral area.
2. Divide by the base perimeter.

::solution
The two bases have area $80\ \text{cm}^2$, so the lateral area is $248-80=168\ \text{cm}^2$. The base perimeter is $28$ cm, giving height $168\div28=6$ cm.

**Answer.** $6$ cm.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
A cuboid is $6$ cm long, $4$ cm wide and $5$ cm high. Its lateral surface area, in $\text{cm}^2$, is
A. $100$.
B. $48$.
C. $24$.
D. $120$.
::key A
We have $(6+4)\times2\times5=100\ \text{cm}^2$.

::mc
A cuboid is $8$ cm long, $3$ cm wide and $2$ cm high. Its total surface area, in $\text{cm}^2$, is
A. $44$.
B. $92$.
C. $68$.
D. $88$.
::key B
We have $44+48=92\ \text{cm}^2$.

::mc
A cuboid has a base perimeter of $30$ cm and a height of $7$ cm. Its lateral surface area, in $\text{cm}^2$, is
A. $37$.
B. $105$.
C. $210$.
D. $420$.
::key C
We have $30\times7=210\ \text{cm}^2$.

::mc
A label covers the four side faces of a box $9$ cm long, $5$ cm wide and $12$ cm high. Its area, in $\text{cm}^2$, is
A. $108$.
B. $168$.
C. $444$.
D. $336$.
::key D
We have $(9+5)\times2\times12=336\ \text{cm}^2$.

::tf
A cuboid is $10$ cm long, $6$ cm wide and $4$ cm high.
a) The perimeter of its base is $32$ cm.
b) The area of one base is $60\ \text{cm}^2$.
c) Its lateral surface area is $128\ \text{cm}^2$.
d) Its total surface area is $188\ \text{cm}^2$.
::key T T T F
a) True since $(10+6)\times2=32$. b) True since $10\times6=60$. c) True since $32\times4=128$. d) False: the correct total is $248\ \text{cm}^2$.

::sa
A cuboid is $7$ cm long and $3$ cm wide. Its lateral surface area is $120\ \text{cm}^2$. What is its height in centimetres?
::key 6
The base perimeter is $(7+3)\times2=20$ cm, so the height is $120\div20=6$ cm.

::sa
A cuboid is $5$ cm long, $4$ cm wide and $3$ cm high. What is its total surface area in square centimetres?
::key 94
The lateral area is $(5+4)\times2\times3=54\ \text{cm}^2$ and the two bases have area $40\ \text{cm}^2$, giving $94\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
