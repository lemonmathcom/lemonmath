## How is each exam covered?

Each exam above is its own roadmap, and every roadmap follows the same order, so you never have to guess what to study first:

1. **Know the exam.** The opening lesson explains the format, the scoring, where a calculator is allowed and a week-by-week revision plan.
2. **Revise by topic.** Each topic has a short theory summary, worked examples written in that exam's question style, similar exercises (follow the model) and application exercises (new situations and real-life problems).
3. **Mini test.** Every lesson ends with a test that is marked at once, so you know whether the topic is secure.
4. **Practice papers.** Full-length papers in the real structure, with answers and a solution to every question.

:::tip Where should I start?
Read [Revision that works](lesson:evidence-based-revision) first. It takes about 25 minutes and makes every later hour of revision count for more.
:::

## Two languages, an advantage for international exams

Every lesson exists in Vietnamese and in English with the same numbers. If you study in the Vietnamese system and are preparing for the SAT, AP or IB, read the Vietnamese version to understand the idea, then switch to the English version to learn the vocabulary and the way real questions are worded.

## Where does the exam information come from?

Formats, timings and dates on this site were checked against official sources (College Board, ACT, the IB, Cambridge International, Vietnam's Ministry and Departments of Education and Training) in November 2026. These organisations may adjust their exams from year to year, so look at the official exam page again before you register. Every question on Lemonmath is written by Lemonmath and is not copied from real exam papers.
