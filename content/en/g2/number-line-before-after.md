In the school playground there is a chalk line with marks spaced evenly apart, labelled 0, 1, 2, …, 20. An stands on mark 7, jumps 5 marks to the right, and then jumps 3 marks to the left.

Which mark will An land on? And how can that chalk line help you add, subtract, and name the numbers that sit next to each other?

## Number lines

An's chalk line is a **number line**. The marks are evenly spaced, with smaller numbers on the left and larger numbers on the right.

:::definition Adding and subtracting on a number line
- Jump to the right: the number **gets bigger**, so we add.
- Jump to the left: the number **gets smaller**, so we subtract.
- A number on the left is smaller than a number on its right.
:::

Why is jumping right the same as adding? The marks are evenly spaced and differ by 1, so jumping 5 marks to the right adds 5.

:::example An jumps along the number line
An stands on mark 7, jumps 5 marks to the right and then 3 marks to the left. Which mark does An land on?

**Solution.** Jumping 5 marks to the right is adding 5, and jumping 3 marks to the left is subtracting 3:
$$7+5=12,\qquad 12-3=9.$$
So An lands on mark 9.
:::

## The number before and the number after

Two numbers next to each other on a number line differ by 1.

:::definition The number before and the number after
- The **number after** a given number is 1 more than it and sits just to its right.
- The **number before** a given number is 1 less than it and sits just to its left.
:::

To find the number after, add 1. To find the number before, subtract 1. When you pass a multiple of ten, the tens digit changes too.

:::example Passing a multiple of ten
Find the number before 49 and the number after 49.

**Solution.** We have:
$$49-1=48,\qquad 49+1=50.$$
So the number before 49 is 48 and the number after 49 is 50.
:::

Now use two neighbouring numbers to solve a problem about your class.

:::example Lan's place in the line
Suppose your class lines up by number. Lan is number 24. Which numbers are the children standing just in front of Lan and just behind her?

**Solution.** The child just in front has the number before 24, and the child just behind has the number after 24:
$$24-1=23,\qquad 24+1=25.$$
So the child just in front of Lan is number 23 and the child just behind her is number 25.
:::

## Parts of addition and subtraction

Every number in an addition and a subtraction has its own name.

:::definition Names of the parts
In the addition $35+24=59$: $35$ and $24$ are the **addends**, and $59$ is the **sum**.

In the subtraction $68-23=45$: $68$ is the **minuend** (the number we take away from), $23$ is the **subtrahend** (the number we take away), and $45$ is the **difference**.
:::

Notice that the sum is greater than each addend, and the difference is smaller than the minuend. The names help you read a question clearly: "find the sum" means add, and "find the difference" means subtract.

:::example Naming the parts
Name each number in the calculations $36+42=78$ and $98-35=63$.

**Solution.** In the addition $36+42=78$: $36$ and $42$ are the addends and $78$ is the sum. In the subtraction $98-35=63$: $98$ is the minuend, $35$ is the subtrahend and $63$ is the difference.
:::

Finally, use the names to solve a problem about a shop.

:::example The exercise-book shop (Apply it)
Suppose a shop sells 45 exercise books in the morning and 32 in the afternoon. Find the total number of books sold in the two parts of the day. Find the difference between the morning and afternoon numbers.

**Understand.** We know 45 books were sold in the morning and 32 in the afternoon. We are asked for the sum and the difference of these two numbers.

**Plan.** The sum is found by adding the two addends, and the difference by subtracting the smaller number from the larger.

**Solve.**

Solution

The total number of books sold in the two parts of the day is:
$$45+32=77\ \text{(books)}.$$
The difference between the morning and afternoon numbers is:
$$45-32=13\ \text{(books)}.$$
Answer: the sum is 77 books and the difference is 13 books.

**Look back.** Is there a way to check the sum? Subtract one addend from the sum and you get the other addend: $77-45=32$, which is correct.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Mixing up the number before and the number after.** Wrong: the number before 40 is 41. Why it is wrong: the number before is 1 less. Right: the number before 40 is 39.
- **Naming the parts of a subtraction wrongly.** Wrong: in $98-35=63$, the number $63$ is the minuend. Why it is wrong: the minuend is the number at the start that we take away from, which is $98$. Right: $98$ is the minuend, $35$ is the subtrahend and $63$ is the difference.
- **Jumping the wrong way on the number line.** Wrong: $12-3$, jump 3 marks to the right to get 15. Why it is wrong: subtracting makes the number smaller, so jump to the left. Right: $12-3=9$.
:::

## Summary

- On a number line, smaller numbers are on the left and larger numbers on the right. Jumping right is adding and jumping left is subtracting.
- For the number after, add 1. For the number before, subtract 1.
- Addition: addend + addend = **sum**. Subtraction: **minuend** − **subtrahend** = **difference**.
- When a question says "find the sum", add. When it says "find the difference", subtract.

Remember the lesson on length. A red ribbon is 36 cm long and a blue ribbon is 21 cm long. Find the total length and the difference in length of the two ribbons.

The total length of the two ribbons is:

:::exercise Challenge
Which number has a number before and a number after that add up to 20?

::hint
**What you need:** the number before is 1 less than the number, and the number after is 1 more.

**Steps:**
1. How far is each of the two neighbouring numbers from the number you are looking for, on either side?
2. Try a few numbers, for example 9, 10 and 11.

::solution
Try 10: the number before is 9, the number after is 11, and $9+11=20$. So the number is 10.

The number before is 1 less and the number after is 1 more, so together they make twice the number. Twice the number is 20, so the number is 10.

**Answer.** 10.
:::
