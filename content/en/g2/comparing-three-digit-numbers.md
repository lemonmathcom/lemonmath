:::goals
- Compare two three-digit numbers by comparing the places from left to right.
- Use the signs $>$, $<$ and $=$ correctly.
- Put three-digit numbers in order, smallest first and largest first.
:::

School A has $428$ pupils. School B has $482$ pupils. Which school has more pupils?

You know how to write a three-digit number as a sum. This lesson shows you how to compare two three-digit numbers quickly and correctly.

## Let's explore

We write both numbers as sums:
$$428=400+20+8,\qquad 482=400+80+2.$$

Both have $4$ hundreds. We compare the tens next: $2$ tens is less than $8$ tens. So $428<482$.

On a number line, the number further to the right is greater:

:::theorem How to compare two three-digit numbers
Compare the places from left to right:
1. Compare the hundreds. The number with more hundreds is greater.
2. If the hundreds are equal, compare the tens.
3. If the tens are also equal, compare the ones.
4. If all three places are equal, the numbers are equal.
:::

We start with the hundreds because one hundred is more than any tens or ones that go with it: $1$ hundred is $10$ tens, which is more than $9$ tens.

## Examples

We start with two numbers that differ in the hundreds.

:::example Comparing the hundreds
Compare $476$ and $534$.

**Solution.** Hundreds: $4$ and $5$. Since $4<5$, the first number is less, and we do not need the tens or ones:
$$476<534.$$
So $476<534$.
:::

Now two numbers with the same hundreds.

:::example Comparing the tens
Compare $352$ and $378$.

**Solution.** Hundreds: $3$ and $3$, equal. Tens: $5$ and $7$. Since $5<7$:
$$352<378.$$
So $352<378$.
:::

Next, two numbers with the same hundreds and the same tens.

:::example Comparing the ones
Compare $641$ and $647$.

**Solution.** Hundreds: $6$ and $6$. Tens: $4$ and $4$. Ones: $1$ and $7$. Since $1<7$:
$$641<647.$$
So $641<647$.
:::

Finally you put several numbers in order.

:::example Putting in order
Put the numbers $325, 235, 253$ in order, smallest first.

**Solution.** Hundreds: $3, 2, 2$. The number $325$ has the most hundreds. The other two have equal hundreds, so compare the tens: $3$ and $5$, so $235<253$:
$$235<253<325.$$
So the order from smallest to largest is $235, 253, 325$.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Comparing the ones first.** Wrong: "$349>351$ because $9>1$". Why it is wrong: we must compare the hundreds first. The hundreds $3$ and $3$ are equal, and the tens $4<5$. Right: $349<351$.
- **Not comparing the next place when the first are equal.** Wrong: "$352=358$ because both start with $35$". Why it is wrong: the ones $2<8$. Right: $352<358$.
- **Turning the sign the wrong way.** Wrong: $476>534$. Why it is wrong: the pointed end faces the smaller number. Right: $476<534$.
:::

## Summary

- Compare place by place from left to right: hundreds, then tens, then ones.
- The first place that differs decides which number is greater.
- The pointed end of $>$ or $<$ faces the smaller number.
- To order smallest first, start with the smallest number.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$ in the box.

a) $243\;\square\;351$.

b) $780\;\square\;620$.

c) $505\;\square\;505$.

::hint
**What you need:** compare the hundreds first.

**Steps:**
1. Look at the hundreds digit of both numbers.
2. If they differ, choose the sign straight away.

::solution
a) $2<3$, so $243<351$. b) $7>6$, so $780>620$. c) The two numbers are the same.

**Answer.** a) $<$; b) $>$; c) $=$.
:::

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$ in the box.

a) $352\;\square\;358$.

b) $910\;\square\;190$.

c) $467\;\square\;427$.

::hint
**What you need:** the first place that differs (from the left) decides.

**Steps:**
1. Compare the hundreds.
2. If equal, compare the tens, then the ones.

::solution
a) Ones: $2<8$. b) Hundreds: $9>1$. c) Tens: $6>2$.

**Answer.** a) $<$; b) $>$; c) $>$.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the last lesson. Write $428$ and $482$ as sums of hundreds, tens and ones, then compare the two numbers.

::hint
**What you need:** $428=400+20+8$.

**Steps:**
1. Write each number as a sum.
2. Compare the hundreds, then the tens.

::solution
$428=400+20+8$ and $482=400+80+2$. The hundreds are equal, and the tens $2<8$.

**Answer.** $428<482$.
:::

:::exercise
Put the numbers in order.

a) Smallest first: $450, 405, 540, 504$.

b) Largest first: $899, 989, 998$.

::hint
**What you need:** compare the hundreds, then the tens, then the ones.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the smallest (or largest) number first.
2. Pick the rest one at a time.

::solution
a) $405<450<504<540$. b) Hundreds: $8, 9, 9$; for the two with hundreds $9$ compare the tens: $8<9$, so $989<998$. So $998>989>899$.

**Answer.** a) $405, 450, 504, 540$; b) $998, 989, 899$.
:::

:::exercise
Which is the greatest of $376, 367, 673$? Why?

::hint
**What you need:** compare the hundreds first.

**Steps:**
1. Write the hundreds of each number.
2. Choose the number with the most hundreds.

::solution
The hundreds are $3, 3, 6$. The number $673$ has the most hundreds.

**Answer.** $673$.
:::

:::exercise
Nam says: "$349>351$ because $9>1$." What is wrong with what Nam says? Correct it.

::hint
**What you need:** we must compare the hundreds first.

**Steps:**
1. Compare the hundreds of the two numbers.
2. If equal, compare the tens.

::solution
The hundreds $3$ and $3$ are equal, and the tens $4<5$. Nam compared the ones first, so he is wrong.

**Answer.** $349<351$.
:::

:::exercise
School A has $428$ pupils and school B has $482$ pupils. Which school has more pupils?

::hint
**What you need:** the greater number means more.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? $428$ pupils and $482$ pupils. What is asked? Which school has more.
2. Compare $428$ and $482$.

::solution
The hundreds are equal and the tens $2<8$, so $428<482$.

**Answer.** School B has more pupils.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Find the digit to put in the box so that $475<4\square5<495$.

::hint
**What you need:** the hundreds are both $4$; look at the tens.

**Steps:**
1. The tens digit must be big enough, but not too big; check the equal cases too.
2. Try the digits $7, 8, 9$.

::solution
Try $\square=7$: $475$ is not greater than $475$. Try $\square=8$: $485$ is greater than $475$ and less than $495$. Try $\square=9$: $495$ is not less than $495$.

**Check.** $475<485<495$. Correct.

**Answer.** $\square=8$.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
Which sign goes in $365\;\square\;358$?
A. $>$.
B. $<$.
C. $=$.
D. They cannot be compared.
::key A
The hundreds are equal and the tens $6>5$, so $365>358$. Option B comes from looking only at the ones.

::mc
Which is the greatest of $504, 540, 450, 405$?
A. $504$.
B. $540$.
C. $450$.
D. $405$.
::key B
The most hundreds is $5$, in $504$ and $540$; the tens $4>0$, so $540$ is the greatest.

::mc
Which is the smallest of $318, 381, 183, 138$?
A. $318$.
B. $381$.
C. $183$.
D. $138$.
::key D
The fewest hundreds is $1$, in $183$ and $138$; the tens $3<8$, so $138$ is the smallest.

::mc
Which number is greater than $628$ and less than $640$?
A. $620$.
B. $635$.
C. $645$.
D. $628$.
::key B
$635>628$ (tens $3>2$) and $635<640$ (tens $3<4$). Option C is greater than $640$.

::tf
Compare the numbers.
a) $700>699$.
b) $456<465$.
c) $349>351$.
d) $510=501$.
::key T T F F
a) True: hundreds $7>6$. b) True: tens $5<6$. c) False: tens $4<5$, so $349<351$. d) False: tens $1>0$, so $510>501$.

::sa
Which is the greatest of $276, 267, 726$?
::key 726
The hundreds digit $7$ is the greatest.

::sa
Which is the smallest of $482, 428, 842$?
::key 428
The fewest hundreds is $4$, in $482$ and $428$; the tens $2<8$.
:::
