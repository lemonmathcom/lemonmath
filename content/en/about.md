## What is Lemonmath?

Lemonmath began with a simple wish: that every student could have a patient teacher sitting beside them, explaining slowly, and carrying on until it makes sense. Not every child has that at home, so we write lessons like that and keep them here, free.

The lessons run from grade 1 to grade 12 and follow the content and order of Vietnam's current mathematics curriculum (the 2018 general education programme, as used in the *Kết nối tri thức* textbook series). Every page comes in **English** and in **Tiếng Việt**, with the same content and the same numbers.

## What is ready now?

- **Grades 9, 10 and 12** have lessons for the whole year.
- **Primary (grades 1–5)** has many lessons, each with a printable self-study sheet.
- **Grades 6, 7, 8 and 11** are being written, a few more lessons every week.
- **Exam prep**: roadmaps for Vietnam's grade 10 entrance and graduation exams, the SAT, ACT, AP and IB.

## How do we write the lessons?

Each lesson follows the way a good teacher works in class. We start from a familiar situation or a small question. We try a few cases together until a pattern shows itself. Only once you can see why does it become a rule. Then come worked examples, the mistakes people often make, and practice with solutions written out in full, the way you would write them in your exercise book.

Every word in the lessons is written by Lemonmath; nothing is copied from a textbook. The curriculum belongs to Vietnam's Ministry of Education and Training, and *Kết nối tri thức* is a textbook series of its publisher. Lemonmath is not affiliated with either.

## Your progress

When you press "Mark as complete", the lesson is remembered on your own device only. Nothing is sent to us, and you do not need an account.

## Spotted a mistake?

Everyone who writes gets something wrong now and then, and we are always grateful to be told. Please [report it here](https://github.com/lemonmathcom/lemonmath/issues) with the name of the lesson and what looks wrong.
