## What is Lemonmath?

Lemonmath is a free website of clear, step-by-step math lessons for students from grade 1 to grade 12. The lessons follow the topics and the order of Vietnam's national mathematics curriculum (the 2018 general education programme, as used in the *Kết nối tri thức* textbook series), and every page is available in **English** and in **Tiếng Việt**.

## What is ready now?

- **Grade 10** is complete: nine chapters and 27 lessons, each with worked examples, pictures, key terms and practice problems with full solutions.
- **Grades 1–9, 11 and 12** show the roadmap of chapters. Their lessons will be added step by step.

## How the lessons are written

Every lesson goes in the same order: a picture or a small example, then the idea in plain words, then the rule, then worked examples, then practice. The text is original; it is not copied from any textbook. The curriculum belongs to the Ministry of Education and Training of Vietnam, and *Kết nối tri thức* is a textbook series of its publisher. Lemonmath is not affiliated with either.

## Your progress

When you press "Mark as complete", the lesson is remembered on your own device only. Nothing is sent to us, and you do not need an account.

## Found a mistake?

We would like to know. Please [report it here](https://github.com/lemonmathcom/lemonmath/issues) and tell us the lesson and what looks wrong.
