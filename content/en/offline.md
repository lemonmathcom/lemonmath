## Why study with the internet off

Parents tell us two stories.

In the first, the family lives where the connection comes and goes, and the page freezes halfway through a lesson. In the second, the connection is excellent, and that is the problem: five minutes into the homework, a video or a game is one tap away.

Both stories end the same way. Once the lessons live on the device, the internet is no longer needed. You can switch the Wi-Fi off for study time, and your child has only the maths in front of them.

## Three steps

1. **Save the lessons while you have Wi-Fi.** In the box above, press **Save** next to your child's grade and keep the page open until the green bar is full. If you only need one chapter, open it and press **Save this chapter**.
2. **Put Lemonmath on the home screen.** On Android, press **Install** when the browser offers it. On an iPhone or iPad, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. From then on Lemonmath opens like an app.
3. **Switch the internet off and start.** Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, or use airplane mode. Open Lemonmath, go to the saved grade and study as usual.

## What works with no connection

- Every lesson of a saved grade, with its pictures, examples and step-by-step solutions.
- Self-marking quizzes and timed exam papers.
- The lesson search box.
- The ticks on lessons you have finished.
- Self-study sheets: they open, so your child can copy the questions into a notebook. Printing needs a printer that is already connected to the device.

Lessons that are not saved are greyed out while you are offline, so it is clear which ones will open.

## Good to know

- **Size.** The number next to each grade is how much data the download uses. On the device the lessons take more room than that, because the formulas are stored ready to display. If space is tight, save one chapter at a time.
- **iPhone and iPad.** Safari may clear saved lessons if the site is not opened for a long time. Adding Lemonmath to the home screen (step 2) keeps them for longer.
- **Updates.** We keep improving the lessons every week. When you are online, come back to this page: a grade with newer lessons shows an **Update** button.
- **Slow connection.** If the network takes too long, the page opens the saved copy after a few seconds, so nobody sits waiting.
- **Removing lessons.** Press **Remove** next to a grade you no longer need to free the space.
- **Privacy.** The lessons are stored on your own device. We cannot see what you saved, and you do not need an account.

## A simple offline routine

Pick a fixed slot, for example 20 minutes after dinner. Switch the Wi-Fi off, open exactly one lesson, read the examples, then try the exercises in a notebook before looking at the solutions. Switch the internet back on when the time is up. Done every day, this builds the habit of staying with one problem, which is worth more than any shortcut.
