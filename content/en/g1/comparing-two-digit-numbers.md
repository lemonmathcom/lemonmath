:::goals
- Compare two two-digit numbers by comparing the tens, then the ones.
- Use the signs $>$, $<$, $=$ correctly.
- Find the greatest and the least number and put numbers in order.
:::

Lan has $35$ marbles and Nam has $42$ marbles. Who has more marbles?

In the last lesson you learned that a two-digit number is made of tens and ones. In this lesson you use the tens and ones to compare two numbers.

## Let's explore

Write the two numbers as tens and ones.

- $35$ is $3$ tens and $5$ ones.
- $42$ is $4$ tens and $2$ ones.

Compare the tens first: $4$ tens are more than $3$ tens. So $42$ is greater than $35$, even though $2$ ones are fewer than $5$ ones.

We write $42>35$, so Nam has more marbles.

:::theorem Comparing two-digit numbers
1. Compare the tens digits first. The number with the greater tens digit is greater.
2. If the tens digits are equal, compare the ones digits. The number with the greater ones digit is greater.
3. If both digits are equal, the numbers are equal.
:::

This works because each ten is $10$ ones, so one more ten is always worth more than a few loose ones.

## Examples

We start with the question from the beginning.

:::example Compare $35$ and $42$
**Solution.** Tens digits: $3$ and $4$. Since $4>3$, we have $42>35$.

So $35<42$.
:::

Now the two numbers have equal tens digits.

:::example Compare $56$ and $52$
**Solution.** The tens digits are both $5$. Compare the ones digits: $6$ and $2$. Since $6>2$, we have $56>52$.

So $56>52$.
:::

Next, put three numbers in order.

:::example Putting numbers in order
Put the numbers $61, 16, 58$ in order from least to greatest.

**Solution.** Compare the tens digits: $1$, $5$, $6$. The number with the least tens digit is $16$, then $58$, then $61$.

So the order is $16, 58, 61$.

**Check.** $16<58$ and $58<61$. Correct.
:::

Last, solve an everyday problem.

:::example Exercise-book labels
Mai has $24$ exercise-book labels and Ha has $27$. Who has more labels?

**Solution.** The tens digits are both $2$. Compare the ones digits: $4$ and $7$. Since $7>4$, we have $27>24$.

So Ha has more labels.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Comparing the ones first.** Wrong: $19>21$ because $9>1$. Why it is wrong: you must compare the tens first, and $1$ ten is less than $2$ tens. Right: $19<21$.
- **Thinking a big digit makes a big number.** Wrong: $38>41$ because it has the digit $8$. Why it is wrong: $3$ tens are less than $4$ tens. Right: $38<41$.
- **Opening the sign the wrong way.** Wrong: $42<35$. Why it is wrong: the mouth must open towards the greater number. Right: $42>35$.
:::

## Summary

- Compare the tens first; the number with the greater tens digit is greater.
- If the tens are equal, compare the ones.
- If both digits are equal, the numbers are equal.
- The signs $>$ and $<$ open towards the greater number.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$: $45 \;\square\; 38$.

::hint
**What you need:** compare the tens first.

**Steps:**
1. What are the tens digits of $45$ and $38$?
2. Which number has the greater tens digit?

::solution
Tens digits: $4>3$, so $45>38$.

**Answer.** $45>38$.
:::

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$: $52 \;\square\; 57$.

::hint
**What you need:** if the tens are equal, compare the ones.

**Steps:**
1. Compare the tens.
2. Compare the ones.

::solution
The tens digits are both $5$. Ones digits: $2<7$, so $52<57$.

**Answer.** $52<57$.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the last lesson: write the number made of $6$ tens and $2$ ones, then compare it with $26$.

::hint
**What you need:** write the tens digit first; compare the tens first.

**Steps:**
1. Write the number $6$ tens $2$ ones.
2. Compare the tens of $62$ and $26$.

::solution
The number is $62$. Tens digits: $6>2$, so $62>26$.

**Answer.** $62>26$.
:::

:::exercise
a) Which number is greater: $73$ or $37$?
b) Which number is less: $91$ or $90$?

::hint
**What you need:** compare the tens; if equal, compare the ones.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: compare the tens $7$ and $3$.
2. Part b: the tens are both $9$, so compare the ones.

::solution
a) $7>3$, so $73$ is greater. b) The tens are equal; $0<1$, so $90$ is less.

**Answer.** a) $73$. b) $90$.
:::

:::exercise
Nam says: "$19>21$ because $9>1$." Is Nam right? Why?

::hint
**What you need:** you must compare the tens first.

**Steps:**
1. What are the tens digits of $19$ and $21$?
2. Which number has the greater tens digit?

::solution
Tens digits: $1<2$, so $19<21$. Nam compared the ones first, so he is wrong.

**Answer.** No. $19<21$.
:::

:::exercise
Lan puts $34, 28, 41$ "from least to greatest" as $41, 34, 28$. Where did Lan go wrong? Put them in the right order.

::hint
**What you need:** from least to greatest means starting with the least number.

**Steps:**
1. Compare the tens digits: $3$, $2$, $4$.
2. Find the least number, then the next.

::solution
Lan put them from greatest to least. The least number is $28$, then $34$, then $41$.

**Answer.** $28, 34, 41$.
:::

:::exercise
The table shows how many pages four children have read:

| Child | An | Binh | Chi | Dung |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pages | $45$ | $54$ | $45$ | $38$ |

a) Who has read the most pages?
b) Who has read the fewest pages?
c) Which two children have read the same number of pages?

::hint
**What you need:** compare the tens first, then the ones.

**Steps:**
1. Read the numbers: $45, 54, 45, 38$.
2. Compare each one.

::solution
a) $54$ has the greatest tens digit, Binh's. b) $38$ has the least tens digit, Dung's. c) An and Chi both read $45$ pages.

**Answer.** a) Binh. b) Dung. c) An and Chi.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Find the digit for the box so that $47<4\square<50$.

::hint
**What you need:** the number has $4$ in the tens place and must be greater than $47$ and less than $50$.

**Steps:**
1. List the numbers greater than $47$ with $4$ tens.
2. Choose those less than $50$.

::solution
The numbers greater than $47$ with $4$ tens are $48$ and $49$. Both are less than $50$.

**Check.** $47<48<50$ and $47<49<50$. Correct.

**Answer.** The digit $8$ or $9$.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
Which sign goes in $62 \;\square\; 59$?
A. $<$.
B. $>$.
C. $=$.
D. cannot tell.
::key B
Tens digits: $6>5$, so $62>59$. Option A is the mistake of comparing the ones first.

::mc
Which sign goes in $47 \;\square\; 47$?
A. $<$.
B. $>$.
C. $=$.
D. cannot tell.
::key C
The tens and the ones are both equal.

::mc
The greatest number in $38, 83, 29, 80$ is
A. $38$.
B. $83$.
C. $29$.
D. $80$.
::key B
The greatest tens digit is $8$, in $83$ and $80$; the ones digit $3>0$, so $83$ is the greatest.

::mc
Putting $25, 52, 22$ in order from least to greatest gives
A. $22, 25, 52$.
B. $52, 25, 22$.
C. $25, 22, 52$.
D. $22, 52, 25$.
::key A
Tens digits: $2, 5, 2$. The numbers $22$ and $25$ both have $2$ tens, and $2<5$ in the ones. Option B is from greatest to least.

::tf
Look at the two numbers $73$ and $37$.
a) $73>37$.
b) $37>73$.
c) $37<73$.
d) $73=37$.
::key T F T F
a) True: the tens digit $7>3$. b) False. c) True. d) False.

::sa
What is the greatest number in $45, 54, 49$?
::key 54
The tens digit $5$ is the greatest, and only $54$ has it.

::sa
What is the least number in $61, 16, 66$?
::key 16
The tens digit $1$ is the least, and only $16$ has it.
:::
