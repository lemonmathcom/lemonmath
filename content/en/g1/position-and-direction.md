:::goals
- Use the words above, below, on the left and on the right to say position.
- Use the words in front of, behind and in the middle to say position in a line.
- Say and understand where things and friends are.
:::

Lan, Mai and Nam line up to go into class. Lan stands in front of Mai, and Mai stands in front of Nam. Who stands in the middle?

You already know some solid shapes. In this lesson you learn to say where things are with words for position.

:::theorem Words for position
1. **Left** and **right**: the hand you write with is your right hand, and the other is your left hand.
2. **Above** and **below**: above is the higher side, below is the lower side.
3. **In front of** and **behind**: in a line, the one nearer the start of the line is in front.
4. **In the middle**: between two things or two friends.
:::

This works because these words tell you which side one thing is on compared with another.

## Examples

We start with the row.

:::example The shape on the left of the circle
In the row (square, circle, triangle from left to right), which shape is on the left of the circle?

**Solution.** Read from left to right: square, circle, triangle. The shape before the circle is the square.

So the square is on the left of the circle.
:::

Next, find the shape in the middle.

:::example The shape in the middle
In the row above, which shape is in the middle?

**Solution.** The row has three shapes. The middle one has a shape on its left and a shape on its right: that is the circle.

So the circle is in the middle.
:::

Now look at the column.

:::example Above and below
In the column (triangle, square, circle from top to bottom), which shape is above the square? Which shape is below the square?

**Solution.** Read from top to bottom: triangle, square, circle. The triangle is above the square and the circle is below the square.

So the triangle is above and the circle is below the square.
:::

Last, answer the question from the start.

:::example Lining up for class
Lan stands in front of Mai, and Mai stands in front of Nam. Who stands in the middle?

**Solution.** The order from the start of the line is: Lan, Mai, Nam. Mai has Lan in front and Nam behind.

So Mai stands in the middle.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Mixing up left and right.** Wrong: the hand you write with is the left hand. Why it is wrong: most children write with the right hand. Right: remember the writing hand is the right hand, the other is the left.
- **Mixing up in front of and behind.** Wrong: the child at the end of the line is in front. Why it is wrong: the child nearer the start is in front. Right: read the order from the start of the line.
- **Mixing up the middle.** Wrong: the shape furthest to the left is in the middle. Why it is wrong: the middle one needs a shape on each side. Right: find the shape with one on its left and one on its right.
:::

## Key terms

:::terms

- on the left | bên trái
- on the right | bên phải
- in front of | trước
- behind | sau
- in the middle | ở giữa
- position | vị trí
:::

## Summary

- Left and right: the writing hand is the right hand.
- Above and below: above is higher, below is lower.
- In front of and behind: read the order from the start of the line.
- In the middle: there is one thing on each side.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
In the row (square, circle, triangle from left to right), which shape is on the right of the circle?

::hint
**What you need:** read from left to right.

**Steps:**
1. Read: square, circle, triangle.
2. Which shape comes after the circle?

::solution
After the circle comes the triangle.

**Answer.** The triangle.
:::

:::exercise
In the column (triangle, square, circle from top to bottom), which shape is at the top?

::hint
**What you need:** read from top to bottom.

**Steps:**
1. Read: triangle, square, circle.
2. Which is the first one?

::solution
The first shape from the top is the triangle.

**Answer.** The triangle.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the last lesson: a dice sits on a table. What solid shape is a dice? What is below the dice?

::hint
**What you need:** a dice has square faces; the thing below is the lower one.

**Steps:**
1. What shape are the faces of a dice?
2. What does the dice sit on?

::solution
A dice is a cube. It sits on the table, so the table is below the dice.

**Answer.** A cube; the table.
:::

:::exercise
In the row (square, circle, triangle from left to right):
a) The circle is on the right of which shape?
b) Is the triangle on the left or on the right of the circle?

::hint
**What you need:** read from left to right; the one that comes later is on the right.

**Steps:**
1. Which shape comes before the circle?
2. Does the triangle come before or after the circle?

::solution
a) The circle is on the right of the square. b) The triangle comes after the circle, so it is on the right.

**Answer.** a) The square. b) On the right.
:::

:::exercise
Binh says: "The square is in the middle" (in the row square, circle, triangle). Is Binh right? Why?

::hint
**What you need:** the shape in the middle has a shape on each side.

**Steps:**
1. Is there a shape on the left of the square?
2. Which shape is in the middle?

::solution
The square is first in the row, with no shape on its left. The shape in the middle is the circle.

**Answer.** No. The circle is in the middle.
:::

:::exercise
Lan stands behind Mai, and Mai stands behind Nam. Lan says: "I am first in the line." Where did Lan go wrong? Who is first?

::hint
**What you need:** the first one has nobody in front.

**Steps:**
1. Write the order from the start of the line.
2. Where is Lan?

::solution
Lan is behind Mai and Mai is behind Nam, so the order is Nam, Mai, Lan. Lan is last.

**Answer.** No. Nam is first and Lan is last.
:::

:::exercise
Three friends sit side by side from left to right: An, Binh, Chi.
a) Who sits in the middle?
b) Who sits on the right of Binh?

::hint
**What you need:** read from left to right; the one in the middle has a friend on each side.

**Steps:**
1. Read: An, Binh, Chi.
2. Find the friend after Binh.

::solution
a) Binh has An on the left and Chi on the right. b) After Binh comes Chi.

**Answer.** a) Binh. b) Chi.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Four friends line up: Ha is in front of Lan, Lan is in front of Mai, Mai is in front of Nam. Who is second from the start? Who is last?

::hint
**What you need:** write the order from the start of the line.

**Steps:**
1. Who is first: the one with nobody in front?
2. Write the others in order.

::solution
The order from the start: Ha, Lan, Mai, Nam.

**Check.** Ha before Lan, Lan before Mai, Mai before Nam. Correct.

**Answer.** Lan is second; Nam is last.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
Three shapes are in a row from left to right: a square, a circle, a triangle. The shape in the middle is the
A. square.
B. circle.
C. triangle.
D. none.
::key B
The circle has the square on its left and the triangle on its right. Option A is the first shape.

::mc
Three shapes are in a column from top to bottom: a triangle, a square, a circle. The shape at the bottom is the
A. triangle.
B. square.
C. circle.
D. none.
::key C
Reading from the top, the last shape is the circle.

::mc
Lan is in front of Mai, and Mai is in front of Nam. Who is in the middle?
A. Lan.
B. Mai.
C. Nam.
D. Nobody.
::key B
The order is Lan, Mai, Nam. Mai is in the middle.

::mc
A book sits on a table. What is below the book?
A. The book.
B. The bag.
C. The table.
D. The pen.
::key C
The book sits on the table, so the table is below the book.

::tf
Three shapes are in a row from left to right: a square, a circle, a triangle.
a) The square is on the left of the circle.
b) The triangle is on the left of the circle.
c) The circle is in the middle.
d) The square is on the right of the triangle.
::key T F T F
a) True. b) False: the triangle is on the right. c) True. d) False: the square is on the left.

::sa
There are $5$ children in a line. Mai is third from the start. How many children are behind Mai?
::key 2
The order is first, second, third (Mai), fourth, fifth. Two children are behind Mai.

::sa
There are $6$ children in a line. Nam is fourth from the start. How many children are in front of Nam?
::key 3
Nam is fourth, so there are three children in front of him.
:::
