:::goals
- Tell the short hand from the long hand on a clock.
- Read the time to the hour, for example 3 o'clock and 9 o'clock.
- Say the seven days of the week in the right order.
- Find yesterday and tomorrow, and count the days between two days of the week.
:::

Suppose that every morning Mai wakes up at 6 o'clock, has breakfast and gets to school at 7 o'clock. A round clock hangs on the wall at home, with two hands and the numbers from 1 to 12. Mai sees that the short hand points to 6, but she does not yet know how to read the clock.

Mai also wants to know what day it is today, because on Saturday her class goes on a trip. How do you read the time to the hour on a clock, and how do the days of the week follow one another?

## Telling the time to the hour

Look at the clock below, which shows 3 o'clock. A clock has two hands: the **short hand** shows the hours and the **long hand** shows the minutes. The face has 12 numbers.

The long hand points exactly to 12 and the short hand points to 3. We read this as **3 o'clock**.

:::theorem Telling the time to the hour
When the long hand points exactly to 12, the number the short hand points to is the time in o'clock.
:::

Why is this true? When the long hand goes once round the clock, 1 hour has passed and the short hand moves on to the next number. Each time the long hand comes back to 12, it is exactly on the hour.

:::example Reading a clock
On a clock the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 9. What time does the clock show?

**Solution.** The long hand points exactly to 12, so we look at the short hand. The short hand points to 9.

So the clock shows 9 o'clock.
:::

Now do it the other way round: you know the time, and you set the hands.

:::example Setting the hands
Where do the short hand and the long hand point when a clock shows 5 o'clock?

**Solution.** On the hour, the long hand points to 12. The hour is 5, so the short hand points to 5.

So the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 5.
:::

You can also use a clock to work out how long something takes.

:::example The coach trip
Suppose the coach taking Mai's class on a trip leaves at 7 o'clock and arrives at 9 o'clock. For how many hours does the coach travel?

**Solution.** From 7 o'clock to 9 o'clock, the short hand moves from 7 to 9. The number of hours is:
$$9-7=2\ \text{(hours)}.$$
So the coach travels for 2 hours.
:::

## Days of the week

A week has **7 days**. The days follow one another in a fixed order and then start again from the beginning.

:::definition The days of the week
The seven days of the week, in order, are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. After Sunday comes Monday of the next week.
- **Today** is the day that is happening now.
- **Yesterday** is the day just before today.
- **Tomorrow** is the day just after today.
:::

Why learn the order? Once you know the order, you can count the days one by one to find out how long you must wait for a particular day. This connects to counting: each day that passes is "add 1".

:::example Yesterday and tomorrow
Today is Wednesday. What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

**Solution.** Yesterday is the day just before Wednesday, and tomorrow is the day just after Wednesday:
$$\text{Tuesday},\ \text{Wednesday},\ \text{Thursday}.$$
So yesterday was Tuesday and tomorrow is Thursday.
:::

Finally, count the days you have to wait.

:::example How many more days? (Apply it)
Suppose Mai's class has a class meeting on Friday. Today is Monday. How many more days until the class meeting?

**Understand.** Today is Monday and the meeting is on Friday. We are asked how many more days.

**Plan.** We count the days after today, one by one, up to Friday.

**Solve.**

Solution

The days after Monday are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. We count 4 days:
$$\text{Monday} \to 1\ (\text{Tuesday}) \to 2 \to 3 \to 4\ (\text{Friday}).$$
Answer: 4 days.

**Look back.** Check with subtraction: if Monday is day number 1 and Friday is day number 5, then $5-1=4$, which is correct. What if today is Saturday and the meeting is on Tuesday? We count: Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, which is 3 days.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Reading the wrong hand.** Wrong: the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 3, and you read 12 o'clock. Why it is wrong: the short hand shows the hour. Right: 3 o'clock.
- **Getting the order of the days wrong.** Wrong: after Friday comes Sunday. Why it is wrong: Saturday is between Friday and Sunday. Right: Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
- **Mixing up yesterday and tomorrow.** Wrong: today is Wednesday, so yesterday is Thursday. Why it is wrong: yesterday has already gone and comes before today. Right: yesterday is Tuesday.
:::

## Summary

- A clock has a short hand that shows the hours and a long hand that shows the minutes, and its face has 12 numbers.
- When the long hand points exactly to 12, the number the short hand points to is the time in o'clock.
- A week has 7 days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
- Yesterday is the day just before today, and tomorrow is the day just after today.
- To find how many more days, count the days one by one in order.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
On a clock the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 8. What time does the clock show?

::hint
**What you need:** when the long hand points to 12, the short hand shows the hour.

**Steps:**
1. Which hand shows the hour?
2. Which number does the short hand point to?

::solution
The short hand points to 8, so the clock shows 8 o'clock.

**Answer.** 8 o'clock.
:::

:::exercise
Where do the long hand and the short hand point when a clock shows 11 o'clock?

::hint
**What you need:** on the hour, the long hand points to 12.

**Steps:**
1. Where does the long hand point on the hour?
2. Which number does the short hand point to?

::solution
On the hour, the long hand points to 12. The short hand points to 11.

**Answer.** The long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 11.
:::

:::exercise
Fill in the missing numbers.

a) 1 hour after 7 o'clock is $\square$ o'clock.

b) 1 hour before 7 o'clock is $\square$ o'clock.

::hint
**What you need:** 1 hour after is add 1, and 1 hour before is subtract 1.

**Steps:**
1. Part a: do you add or subtract?
2. Part b: do you add or subtract?

::solution
a) $7+1=8$.

b) $7-1=6$.

**Answer.** a) 8 o'clock; b) 6 o'clock.
:::

:::exercise
Today is Thursday. What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

::hint
**What you need:** yesterday is just before today, and tomorrow is just after today.

**Steps:**
1. Write the order of the days around Thursday.
2. Find the day before and the day after.

::solution
$$\text{Wednesday},\ \text{Thursday},\ \text{Friday}.$$

**Answer.** Yesterday was Wednesday and tomorrow is Friday.
:::

:::exercise
Suppose the clock in the school playground shows 9 o'clock. What time will it show 3 hours later?

::hint
**What you need:** "3 hours later" means add 3 hours: use addition.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. How do we solve it? Which calculation do you use?
3. Check: when a clock shows 12 o'clock, where does the short hand point?

::solution
Solution

3 hours later, the clock will show:
$$9+3=12\ \text{(o'clock)}.$$
Answer: 12 o'clock.
:::

:::exercise
Suppose the school library is open from Monday to Friday. On how many days a week is the library open?

::hint
**What you need:** count the days in order through the week.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. Write the days from Monday to Friday one by one, then count.

::solution
Solution

The library is open on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. That is 5 days.

Answer: 5 days.
:::

:::exercise
Remember subtraction within 10. A week has 7 days and you go to school on 5 of them. On how many days a week do you not go to school?

::hint
**What you need:** "do not go" means what is left over: use subtraction.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? What are we asked?
2. How do we solve it? Which calculation do you use?
3. Check: is $5+2=7$?

::solution
Solution

The number of days a week you do not go to school is:
$$7-5=2\ \text{(days)}.$$
Answer: 2 days.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Today is Tuesday. Your class plans a field trip in 5 days' time. What day of the week is the trip?

::hint
**What you need:** after Sunday comes Monday of the next week.

**Steps:**
1. Count the days one by one, starting with the day after Tuesday.
2. Which day is the 5th one you count?

::solution
Count the days after Tuesday: Wednesday (1), Thursday (2), Friday (3), Saturday (4), Sunday (5).

**Answer.** Sunday.
:::

## Mini test

:::quiz
::mc
On a clock the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 6. The clock shows
A. 12 o'clock.
B. 5 o'clock.
C. 6 o'clock.
D. 7 o'clock.
::key C
The short hand shows the hour and points to 6, so the clock shows 6 o'clock. Option A reads the long hand by mistake.

::mc
A clock shows 11 o'clock. After another 1 hour, the short hand points to
A. 10.
B. 11.
C. 1.
D. 12.
::key D
After 1 hour the short hand moves to the next number: $11+1=12$. Option A is 1 hour before, and option B is no change.

::mc
Today is Wednesday. Tomorrow is
A. Thursday.
B. Tuesday.
C. Friday.
D. Saturday.
::key A
Tomorrow is the day just after Wednesday, which is Thursday. Option B is yesterday.

::mc
Today is Saturday. Yesterday was
A. Sunday.
B. Friday.
C. Monday.
D. Thursday.
::key B
Yesterday is the day just before Saturday, which is Friday. Option A is tomorrow, and option D is two days ago.

::tf
Mai wakes up at 6 o'clock and gets to school at 7 o'clock.
a) At 7 o'clock, the long hand points to 7.
b) Mai wakes up after she gets to school.
c) At 6 o'clock, the long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 6.
d) From waking up to getting to school is 1 hour.
::key F F T T
a) False: on the hour, the long hand points to 12. b) False: 6 o'clock is earlier than 7 o'clock. c) True: on the hour the long hand points to 12, and the short hand points to 6. d) True: $7-6=1$.

::sa
Today is Monday. How many more days until Friday?
::key 4
The days after Monday are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, which is 4 days.

::sa
Lan studies from 3 o'clock to 5 o'clock. For how many hours does Lan study?
::key 2
$5-3=2$ (hours).
:::
