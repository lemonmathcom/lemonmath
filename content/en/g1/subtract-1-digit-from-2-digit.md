:::goals
- Name the tens and the ones of a two-digit number, for example $27$.
- Subtract a one-digit number from a two-digit number (no regrouping), for example $27-4$.
- Solve a word problem with one subtraction.
:::

Nam has $27$ marbles. He gives $4$ marbles to a friend. How many marbles does Nam have left?

You could count back one at a time: $26, 25, 24, 23$. But counting back many marbles takes a long time. This lesson shows you a quicker way, using tens and ones.

## Let's explore

The number $27$ has $2$ tens and $7$ ones.

- Nam has $2$ tens of marbles and $7$ single marbles.
- He gives away $4$ single marbles. The single marbles left are $7-4=3$.
- He gives away no group of ten, so he still has $2$ tens.

So $2$ tens and $3$ ones make $23$. We have $27-4=23$.

:::theorem How to subtract a one-digit number from a two-digit number
To work out $27-4$:
1. Subtract the ones: $7-4=3$.
2. Keep the tens the same: $2$ tens.
3. Write the answer: $2$ tens and $3$ ones make $23$.
:::

This works because you only take away single marbles. The number of tens does not change, so you keep the tens the same.

## Examples

We start with the simplest subtraction.

:::example Work out $27-4$
**Solution.** $27$ has $2$ tens and $7$ ones. Subtract the ones: $7-4=3$. Keep the tens the same: $2$:
$$27-4=23.$$
So $27-4=23$.
:::

Now a similar subtraction with different numbers.

:::example Work out $58-6$
**Solution.** $58$ has $5$ tens and $8$ ones. Subtract the ones: $8-6=2$. Keep the tens the same: $5$:
$$58-6=52.$$
So $58-6=52$.
:::

When the number you subtract equals the ones digit, no ones are left.

:::example Work out $45-5$
**Solution.** $45$ has $4$ tens and $5$ ones. Subtract the ones: $5-5=0$. Keep the tens the same: $4$:
$$45-5=40.$$
So $45-5=40$.
:::

Now use subtraction to answer a question from everyday life.

:::example On the bus
There are $36$ people on a bus. At a stop, $3$ people get off. How many people are left on the bus?

**Solution.** "Get off" and "left" mean taking away, so we subtract. Subtract the ones: $6-3=3$. Keep the tens the same: $3$:
$$36-3=33\ \text{(people)}.$$
So $33$ people are left on the bus.

**Check.** Check with addition: $33+3=36$. The answer is correct.
:::

:::warning Mistakes to watch out for
- **Forgetting the tens.** Wrong: $27-4=3$. Why it is wrong: you only worked out $7-4$ and forgot the $2$ tens. Right: $27-4=23$.
- **Mixing up subtraction and addition.** Wrong: $27-4=31$. Why it is wrong: you worked out $27+4$. Right: "left" means taking away, so $27-4=23$.
- **No unit in a word problem.** Wrong: "Answer: $33$". Right: "Answer: $33$ people".
:::

## Summary

- To subtract a one-digit number from a two-digit number (no regrouping), subtract the ones and keep the tens the same.
- Example: $27-4=23$ because $7-4=3$ and the tens stay $2$.
- Check with addition: $23+4=27$.
- Questions with "left" or "take away" usually need subtraction; remember the unit in the answer.

## Practice

Each exercise comes with what you need and how to start. Try it yourself first, then open the solution.

:::exercise
Work out $38-5$.

::hint
**What you need:** subtract the ones, keep the tens the same.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $8-5$.
2. The tens stay $3$.

::solution
$8-5=3$ and the tens are $3$, so $38-5=33$.

**Answer.** $33$.
:::

:::exercise
Work out $79-6$.

::hint
**What you need:** $79$ has $7$ tens and $9$ ones.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $9-6$.
2. Keep the $7$ tens.

::solution
$9-6=3$ and the tens are $7$, so $79-6=73$.

**Answer.** $73$.
:::

:::exercise
Work out $64-4$.

::hint
**What you need:** if you subtract the ones digit, no ones are left.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $4-4$.
2. Keep the $6$ tens.

::solution
$4-4=0$ and the tens are $6$, so $64-4=60$.

**Answer.** $60$.
:::

:::exercise
Write $>$, $<$ or $=$ in the box: $48-5 \;\square\; 40$.

::hint
**What you need:** work out the difference first, then compare the two numbers.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $48-5$.
2. Compare the result with $40$.

::solution
$8-5=3$, so $48-5=43$. Since $43>40$, we have $48-5>40$.

**Answer.** The sign $>$.
:::

:::exercise
A box has $45$ sweets. You eat $3$ of them. How many sweets are left in the box?

::hint
**What you need:** "eat" and "left" mean taking away: use subtraction.

**Steps:**
1. What do we know? $45$ sweets and $3$ are eaten. What is asked? How many are left.
2. Work out $45-3$ and write the answer with its unit.

::solution
The number of sweets left is:
$$45-3=42\ \text{(sweets)}.$$

**Answer.** $42$ sweets.
:::

:::exercise
Remember the last lesson: adding two two-digit numbers. Work out $32+45$, then subtract $6$ from the result.

::hint
**What you need:** add the ones to the ones and the tens to the tens; then subtract the ones.

**Steps:**
1. Work out $32+45$.
2. Subtract $6$ from the result.

::solution
$32+45=77$. Then $7-6=1$ and the tens are $7$, so $77-6=71$.

**Answer.** $71$.
:::

:::exercise
Lan writes: $46-3=16$. Is Lan right or wrong? Why? Correct it.

::hint
**What you need:** a one-digit number is subtracted from the ones only.

**Steps:**
1. Which place did Lan subtract $3$ from?
2. Work out $6-3$ again and keep the tens the same.

::solution
Lan is wrong, because she took $3$ from the tens ($4$ tens became $1$ ten). The right way: $6-3=3$ and the tens are $4$, so $46-3=43$.

**Answer.** Wrong; the correct answer is $43$.
:::

:::exercise Challenge
Find the missing number: $\square-5=32$.

::hint
**What you need:** the ones digit of the difference is $2$. Which number minus $5$ makes $2$?

**Steps:**
1. Ask: $?-5=2$, so $?=7$.
2. The tens stay $3$.

::solution
Ones: $7-5=2$. The tens are $3$, so the missing number is $37$.

**Check.** $37-5=32$. Correct.

**Answer.** $37$.
:::

::key B

A. $57-6$.
B. $59-6$.

B. $34$ stamps.
C. $4$ stamps.
D. $24$ stamps.
::key D
"Gives" and "left" mean taking away: $29-5=24$. Option B is $29+5$, an addition by mistake.

::tf
Look at the subtraction $76-4$.
a) The number $76$ has $7$ tens.
b) The ones: $6-4=2$.
c) $76-4=52$.
d) $76-4$ is less than $75$.
::key T T F T
a) True: $76$ has $7$ tens and $6$ ones. b) True: $6-4=2$. c) False: the tens stay $7$, so $76-4=72$. d) True: $72<75$.

::sa

::key 82
$9-7=2$ and the tens are $8$, so $89-7=82$.

::key 52
$58-6=52$ (fruits).
