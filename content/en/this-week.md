## For parents: adding with regrouping, grade 2

Lesson: [Adding a one-digit number to a two-digit number (with regrouping)](lesson:regroup-add-1-digit).

The most common mistake is **forgetting the carried ten**: a child writes $38+6=34$ instead of $44$. A 10-minute way to help:

1. Ask your child to make $38$ with $3$ bundles of ten sticks and $8$ single sticks, then add $6$ more sticks.
2. Ask: "Can $14$ single sticks make one more bundle of ten?"
3. Print the lesson's self-study sheet, let your child work alone, then look at the **Mistakes to watch out for** box together.
