## Run a test live for the whole class

The test builder above has a button, **Run it live for the class**. Choose lessons and the number of questions for each part (multiple choice, true/false, short answer), then press it. Marks follow the graduation exam and are scaled to 10. The screen switches to a view for the projector showing an address, a QR code and a 6-digit class code.

1. Students open the address on their phones and enter the class code and a name. Each name appears on the projector as the student joins.
2. Set the time and press **Start**. The whole class works at once, and each answered question lights up on the projector.
3. When time runs out, or when you press **End**, the projector shows each student’s score, the class mean and the most missed questions. Click a question number to show the question and what the class chose.
4. On the phone, each student gets a score, feedback from Lemon and an explanation of every question.

While the test is running the projector only shows which questions have been answered, not whether they are right. You can switch on **Show right/wrong** or **Hide names** at any time. Afterwards, **Write notes to parents** sends the scores to the score analyser above.

## Other things you can use straight away

Besides the two tools above, the whole site can be used in class or set as homework.

- **Printable self-study sheets.** Every lesson has an A4 sheet with a summary, exercises and space for the working. Open a lesson and press the print-sheet button at the top.
- **A mini test at the end of every lesson.** Students take it at home and it is marked on submit, with feedback, likely causes of lost marks and next steps. You only need to send the class the link.
- **Past papers and exam prep.** Timed papers with automatic marking of the objective part and a printable version with answers. See the [exam prep section](exam:).
- **Lemon Arena on the projector.** In the [Arena](../arena/), press "Open a room", untick "I play too" and put the screen up: the class joins with a 6-digit code, everyone answers the same mental-maths or grade-knowledge questions, and the scoreboard runs live. Good for a five-minute warm-up. A quick wrong answer loses points, so students learn to be sure first and fast second.
- **Study groups.** Suggest that each table starts a [study group](../study-groups/) with a shared weekly goal; students cheer each other on without you having to manage anything.
- **Lessons in two languages.** Every lesson exists in English and in Vietnamese, which helps classes that teach maths in English.

## Lemonmath and independent study

Lemonmath exists to help students study better on their own. It does not replace your teaching. The pattern we find works best: set a lesson to read at home, let students take the mini test, then spend class time on what they found hard.

## About data

The tests you build and the scores you type into the analyser are processed on your own device only. A live class test is the exception: the names students enter and their answers are held on a server while the class is open, and deleted when you press Close class. You may ask students to enter a nickname or a number.

For My classes (homework with a time window), the class list, each student’s personal code and their answers are kept on Lemonmath’s server to mark the work and report to you; only your account can see them. Answers are deleted 30 days after an assignment closes. When you delete a class or your whole account, everything that belongs to it is deleted too. The homework page shows students only their number and given name, never full names.
