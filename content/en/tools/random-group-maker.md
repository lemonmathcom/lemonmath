## How to use it

1. Paste your class list (one name per line) or choose a saved class. The lists are shared with the [Random Name Picker](../random-name-picker/).
2. Choose **By number of groups** (say 6 groups) or **By students per group** (say at most 4), type the number and press **Make groups**.
3. To swap someone: drag the name to another group, or tap the name and then **Move here** in the new group (this also works on phones).
4. Click a group's name to rename it. Then copy the groups into your class chat, export a CSV file, print them, or show them full screen.

## How are the groups kept even?

Group sizes never differ by more than one, and every student is in exactly one group. A class of 38 in 6 groups becomes 2 groups of 7 and 4 groups of 6. By students per group, no group is bigger than the number you chose: 10 students, at most 4 per group, gives 4, 3, 3 rather than 4, 4, 2.

The order is shuffled with the browser's secure random number generator, so every split is equally likely. You can still move a few students so each group has someone to lead the way.

## Ideas for maths lessons

- **Learning stations:** one type of problem per group, 8 minutes on the [Classroom Timer](../classroom-timer/), then rotate.
- **Practice pairs:** choose **By students per group** and type 2 to get random pairs who solve and check each other's work.
- **Team challenge:** once the groups are made, open a room in the [Lemon Arena](../../arena/) so the whole class answers the same set of questions.
