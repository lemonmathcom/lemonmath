## How to use it

1. Paste your class list into **Students**, one name per line. You can copy a whole column of names from Excel, Word or a chat; numbers, dates of birth and gender columns that come with it are dropped. To use a file you already have, press **Import a CSV file**.
2. Choose **Wheel** (good on a projector, up to 60 names) or **Quick pick** (several students at once, good for big classes).
3. Press **Pick a name**, or press **Space** while it is in full screen.

Each class keeps its own list, history and who has been picked. Press **+ New class** for another class; the lists are shared with the [Random Group Maker](../random-group-maker/).

## Is it really fair?

Yes. The name is drawn first with the browser's secure random number generator, so every student still on the list has the same chance; the wheel only turns to the name that was drawn. With **Do not pick the same student twice** on, nobody comes up again until the whole class has had a turn, or until you press **Start a new round**.

## Ideas for maths lessons

- **Questions at the start of the lesson:** random picking means everyone prepares, because anyone can be asked.
- **Working at the board:** switch to **Quick pick** and choose 3 students to solve three questions side by side.
- **Wait, then pick:** ask the question, give the class 30 seconds on the [Classroom Timer](../classroom-timer/), then spin. Everyone thinks, not only the quickest hands.

## Does my class list go anywhere?

No. The list, the history and the settings stay in this browser on this device. Delete them with the button at the bottom of the page, or export a CSV file to take them to another computer.
